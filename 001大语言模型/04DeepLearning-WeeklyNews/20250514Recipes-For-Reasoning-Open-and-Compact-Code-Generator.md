Dear friends,

AI's ability to make tasks not just cheaper，but also faster，is underrated in its importance in creating business value.

AI 不仅能让任务成本更低，还能让它们更快完成，这项能力在创造商业价值方面的重要性被低估了。

For the task of writing code，AI is a game-changer. It takes so much less effort — and is so much cheaper — to write software with AI assistance than without. But beyond reducing the cost of writing software，AI is shortening the time from idea to working prototype，and the ability to test ideas faster is changing how teams explore and invent. When you can test 20 ideas per month，it dramatically changes what you can do compared to testing 1 idea per month. This is a benefit that comes from AI-enabled speed rather than AI-enabled cost reduction.

对于编写代码这项任务，AI 是一个颠覆者。与没有 AI 辅助相比，使用 AI 辅助编写软件所需的努力少得多，成本也低得多。但除了降低编写软件的成本，AI 正在缩短从想法到工作原型的时间，而更快地测试想法的能力正在改变团队探索和发明的方式。当你每月可以测试 20 个想法时，与每月测试 1 个想法相比，这极大地提升了你的能力。这项优势来自于 AI 带来的速度，而非成本降低。

That AI-enabled automation can reduce costs is well understood. For example，providing automated customer service is cheaper than operating human-staffed call centers. Many businesses are more willing to invest in growth than just in cost savings; and，when a task becomes cheaper，some businesses will do a lot more of it，thus creating growth. But another recipe for growth is underrated：Making certain tasks much faster（whether or not they also become cheaper）can create significant new value.

人工智能自动化（AI-enabled automation）可以降低成本，这一点是众所周知的。例如，提供自动化客户服务比运营人工座席的呼叫中心更便宜。许多企业更愿意投资于增长而不仅仅是节省成本；而且，当一项任务变得更便宜时，一些企业会做更多这样的任务，从而创造增长。但是，另一种增长的方法被低估了：使某些任务变得更快（无论它们是否也变得更便宜）可以创造显著的新价值。

I see this pattern across more and more businesses. Consider the following scenarios:

我观察到越来越多的企业正在采纳这种模式。考虑以下场景：

1 If a lender can approve loans in minutes using AI，rather than days waiting for a human to review them，this creates more borrowing opportunities（and also lets the lender deploy its capital faster). Even if human-in-the-loop review is needed，using AI to get the most important information to the reviewer might speed things up. The ability to provide loans quickly opens up the market to new customers in need of rapid funds and helps customers who need a quick positive or negative decision to accept the loan or move on.

如果一家贷款机构可以使用 AI 在几分钟内批准贷款，而不是等待人工审核几天，这会创造更多的借贷机会（也让贷款机构更快地部署其资本）。即使需要人工复核，使用 AI 向审核人员提供最重要的信息也可能会加快速度。快速提供贷款的能力为需要快速资金的新客户打开了市场，并帮助客户快速获得肯定或否定的决定，以便决定是否接受贷款或寻找其他方案。

2 If an academic institution gives homework feedback to students in minutes（via sophisticated autograding）rather than days（via human grading），not only is the automation cheaper，the rapid feedback facilitates better learning.

如果一个学术机构能在几分钟内（借助复杂的自动评分系统）而不是几天内（通过人工评分）就给学生提供作业反馈，那么自动化不仅能降低成本，这种快速反馈还能促进更好的学习。

3 If an online seller can approve purchases faster，this can lead to more sales. For example，many platforms that accept online ad purchases have an approval process that can take hours or days; if approvals can be done faster，they can earn revenue faster. Further，for customers buying ads，being able to post an ad in minutes lets them test ideas faster and also makes the ad product more valuable.

如果在线卖家能更快地审核购买，这有助于提升销量。例如，许多接受在线广告投放的平台都有审批流程，这个过程可能需要数小时甚至数天；如果能加快审批速度，就能更快地带来收益。此外，对于购买广告的客户来说，能够在几分钟内投放广告，不仅让他们能更快地测试创意，也让广告产品本身更具吸引力。

4 If a company's sales department can prioritize leads and respond to prospective customers in minutes or hours rather than days — closer to when the customers' buying intent first led them to contact the company — sales representatives might close more deals. Likewise，a business that can respond more quickly to requests for proposals may win more deals.

如果一家公司的销售部门能够在几分钟或几小时内（而不是几天）优先处理销售线索并快速回应潜在客户 —— 尤其是在客户最初产生购买意向并主动联系公司的时候 —— 销售代表就可能促成更多交易。同理，一家能更快响应投标请求的企业，也更有机会赢得业务。

I've written previously about looking at the tasks a company does to explore where AI can help. Many teams already do this with an eye toward making tasks cheaper，either to save costs or to do those tasks many more times. If you're doing this exercise，consider also whether AI can significantly speed up certain tasks. One place to examine is the sequence of tasks on the path to earning revenue. If some of the steps can be sped up，perhaps this can help revenue growth.

我之前曾写过文章，探讨如何审视一家公司的各项任务，从而发掘人工智能（AI）的用武之地。许多团队在做这项工作时，已经会考虑如何降低任务成本，这样做或是为了节约开支，或是为了能更频繁地执行这些任务。如果你也在进行这样的审视，不妨再思考一下，AI 是否能够显著提升某些任务的速度。一个值得关注的环节，就是公司赚取收入流程中的一系列任务。如果其中的某些步骤能够提速，或许就能助推营收增长。

Growth is more interesting to most businesses than cost savings，and if there are loops in your business that，when sped up，would drive growth，AI might be a tool to unlock this growth.

对于大多数企业而言，增长比节省成本更具吸引力。如果你的业务中存在一些通过加速特定环节就能实现增长的「循环」，那么 AI 可能就是释放这种增长潜力的关键工具。

Keep building!

Andrew

#### Reasoning Models With Recipes

推理模型的「秘诀」

Microsoft published its latest recipe for training reasoning models，substantially expanding what is still a fairly small base of public knowledge.

Microsoft 发布了其最新的用于训练推理模型的方法，极大地丰富了目前仍相对较小的公共知识库。

What's new: Microsoft released Phi-4-reasoning，Phi-4-reasoning-plus and Phi-4-mini-reasoning along with lessons learned in building the models.

新的内容：Microsoft 发布了 Phi-4-reasoning、Phi-4-reasoning-plus 和 Phi-4-mini-reasoning 模型，并分享了构建这些模型过程中积累的经验。

1 Input/output：text in（Phi-4-reasoning up to 32,000 tokens，Phi–4-reasoning-plus up to 32,000 tokens，Phi-4-mini-reasoning up to 128,000 tokens），text out

输入 / 输出：文本输入（Phi-4-reasoning 最高支持 32,000 tokens，Phi–4-reasoning-plus 最高支持 32,000 tokens，Phi-4-mini-reasoning 最高支持 128,000 tokens），文本输出

2 Architecture：Transformer（Phi-4-reasoning 14 billion parameters，Phi-4-reasoning-plus 14 billion parameters，Phi-4-mini-reasoning：3.8 billion parameters)

架构：Transformer （Phi-4-reasoning 拥有 140 亿参数，Phi-4-reasoning-plus 拥有 140 亿参数，Phi-4-mini-reasoning：拥有 38 亿参数)

3 Features：Reasoning

特性：推理（Reasoning)

4 Performance：Phi-4-reasoning-plus and Phi-4-mini-reasoning perform well on math problems

性能：Phi-4-reasoning-plus 和 Phi-4-mini-reasoning 在数学问题上表现良好

5 Availability：Weights free to download for noncommercial and commercial uses under an MIT license

可用性：模型权重可免费下载，用于非商业和商业目的，采用 MIT 许可协议

How it works: All three models are fine-tuned versions of pretrained models.

工作原理：所有这三个模型都是在预训练模型基础上进行微调的版本。

1 Phi-4-reasoning：The authors fine-tuned Phi-4 to match curated outputs from OpenAI o3-mini on Q&A，math，science，and coding examples.

Phi-4-reasoning：作者们对 Phi-4 进行了精调，使其在问答、数学、科学和编码等示例上，能够匹配 OpenAI o3-mini 生成的经过精心筛选的输出。

2 Phi-4-reasoning-plus：They further fine-tuned Phi-4-reasoning via reinforcement learning to correctly answer math problems.

Phi-4-reasoning-plus：他们通过强化学习进一步优化了 Phi-4-reasoning 模型，使其能够更准确地回答数学问题。

3 Phi-4-mini-reasoning：They fine-tuned Phi-4-mini in stages to reason over math problems. Stages included（i）supervised fine-tuning to match correct output from DeepSeek-R1，(ii）direct preference optimization to train the model to prefer correct responses over incorrect ones from DeepSeek-R1，and（iii）reinforcement learning to further reward correct solutions to math problems.

Phi-4-mini-reasoning：他们分阶段对 Phi-4-mini 进行了微调，使其能够对数学问题进行推理。这些阶段包括： （i）进行有监督微调，让其输出与 DeepSeek-R1 的正确答案一致；（ii）采用直接偏好优化，训练模型更倾向于选择 DeepSeek-R1 中的正确答案而非错误答案；以及 （iii）运用强化学习，进一步奖励数学问题的正确解答。

Smaller model lessons learned: During reinforcement learning，Phi-4-mini-reasoning exhibited instability，such as output batches that varied greatly in length or received mostly negative rewards，apparently depending on the training data or output. The authors suspect that the model's small size caused these issues. Among the lessons learned:

更小模型的经验教训：在强化学习期间，Phi-4-mini-reasoning 表现出不稳定性，例如输出的批次长度差异很大，或者获得的奖励大多是负面的，这显然与训练数据或输出有关。作者怀疑，模型尺寸过小是导致这些问题的原因。从中得到的经验教训是：

1 Supervised fine-tuning on existing reasoning datasets like S1K can decrease performance. This phenomenon suggests a need either for larger，high-quality，supervised fine-tuning datasets or for fine-tuning via both supervised learning and reinforcement learning.

对现有推理数据集，如 S1K，进行监督微调，反而可能降低性能。这一现象提示我们，也许需要更大、更高质量的监督微调数据集，或者可以考虑结合监督学习和强化学习来共同进行微调。

2 To minimize discrepancies in output length，the authors tested multiple prompts and chose those that resulted in the most uniform output lengths.

为了尽量减少输出长度的差异，作者测试了多个提示（prompt），并从中选择了那些能产生最一致输出长度的提示。

3 To address the output batches that received mostly negative rewards，they sampled lots of responses，retained those that received a positive reward，sampled an equal number of those that received a negative reward，and discarded the rest before adjusting the model's weights.

为了处理那些大部分都收到负面奖励的输出批次，他们采样了大量的响应，保留了那些收到正面奖励的，并采样了同样数量的收到负面奖励的，然后在调整模型权重之前丢弃了其余的响应。

Larger model lessons learned: Phi-4-reasoning and Phi-4-reasoning-plus didn't present the same issues. However，the authors did make significant choices during reinforcement learning:

关于更大模型的经验教训：Phi-4-reasoning 和 Phi-4-reasoning-plus 没有出现同样的问题。然而，作者在强化学习过程中确实做出了重要的选择：

1 They fine-tuned Phi-4-reasoning on both math and code data，but during reinforcement learning，they fine-tuned it only on math data to simplify the training process. The authors attribute the model's relatively lackluster performance on code benchmarks to this choice.

研究人员在数学和代码数据上都对 Phi-4-reasoning 进行了微调（fine-tuned），但在强化学习（reinforcement learning）阶段，为了简化训练过程，他们只用数学数据进行了微调。作者认为，模型在代码基准测试上表现相对不太理想，原因可能就在于此。

2 They crafted the reward function to give lower rewards for correct responses longer than 25,600 tokens than for shorter responses. This encouraged the model to finish thinking within the input length. Furthermore，the reward function gave a greater punishment for incorrect responses with fewer than 3,702 tokens compared to longer responses. This encouraged the model to produce more reasoning tokens when solving hard problems.

他们精心设计了奖励函数，对于超过 25,600 个 token 的正确回答，给出的奖励会比短回答低。这样做是为了鼓励模型在规定的输入长度内完成思考。此外，与较长的错误回答相比，奖励函数对于少于 3,702 个 token 的错误回答会施加更大的惩罚。这有助于促使模型在解决困难问题时，输出更多的推理过程 token。

Results: Overall，Phi-4-reasoning-plus and Phi-4-mini-reasoning outperform similarly sized（and larger）open-weights models on math problems. Phi-4-reasoning generally outperformed DeepSeek-R1-Distilled-70B but underperformed Alibaba QwQ 32B. All three models deliver performance that falls in the middle among proprietary models and，in domains outside math，larger models with open weights.

结果：总体而言，Phi-4-reasoning-plus 和 Phi-4-mini-reasoning 在数学问题上的表现优于相似大小（以及更大）的开源模型。Phi-4-reasoning 的表现通常优于 DeepSeek-R1-Distilled-70B，但不如阿里巴巴的 QwQ 32B。这三个模型在性能上都处于专有模型性能范围的中间水平，并且在数学以外的领域，性能与更大的开源模型相当。

1 On math problems in AIME 2024，Phi-4-reasoning-plus（81.3 percent accuracy）outperformed the next-best open-weights model，QwQ 32B（79.5 percent accuracy). In comparison，Phi-4-reasoning（74.6 percent accuracy）underperformed the proprietary Gemini 2.5 Pro（92 percent accuracy).

在 AIME 2024 的数学题上，Phi-4-reasoning-plus（81.3% 的准确率）表现优于排名第二的开源模型 QwQ 32B（79.5% 的准确率）。相比之下，Phi-4-reasoning（74.6% 的准确率）表现不及专有的 Gemini 2.5 Pro 模型（92% 的准确率）。

2 On AIME 2024，Phi-4-mini-reasoning（57.5 percent accuracy）outperformed the next-best open-weights model of similar size，DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B（53.3 percent accuracy). In comparison，o1-mini achieved 63.6 percent accuracy.

在 AIME 2024 测试中，Phi-4-mini-reasoning 的准确率达到了 57.5%，优于尺寸相似的下一个表现最好的开源模型 DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B（准确率为 53.3%）。相比之下，o1-mini 的准确率达到了 63.6%。

Why it matters: While reasoning models can outperform their non-reasoning counterparts，the best ways to train them aren't widely known. Sharing recipes and lessons learned enables others to further iterate and improve the recipes，ultimately increasing model performance even more.

重要性：尽管推理模型（reasoning models）的表现优于非推理模型（non-reasoning counterparts），但训练这些模型的最佳方法尚未被广泛知晓。分享训练方法和经验教训，能让其他人在此基础上进一步迭代和改进，最终进一步提升模型的性能。

#### Open，Compact Code Generator

开源、紧凑的代码生成器

An open-source code generator performs comparably to the reasoning models DeepSeek-R1 and OpenAI o1 with a much smaller model.

一个开源的代码生成器，在性能上与 DeepSeek-R1 和 OpenAI o1 等具有推理能力的模型相当，而模型规模却小得多。

What's new: A team at the model platform Together.AI and Agentica，an open-source project devoted to reinforcement learning（RL），released DeepCoder-14B-Preview. The release includes weights，code，dataset，training logs，and data optimizations under an MIT license that allows noncommercial and commercial uses.

最新动态：模型平台 Together.AI 与致力于强化学习（RL）的开源项目 Agentica 联手，一支团队发布了 DeepCoder-14B-Preview 模型。此次发布的内容非常丰富，包括了模型权重、代码、数据集、训练日志以及数据优化方法。所有这些资源都遵循 MIT 许可证，允许非商业和商业用途。

How it works: The team fine-tuned DeepSeek-R1-Distilled-Qwen-14B，which distills knowledge from DeepSeek-R1（671 billion parameters）into Qwen-14B（14 billion parameters).

它是如何工作的：该团队对 DeepSeek-R1-Distilled-Qwen-14B 模型进行了微调，这个模型巧妙地将 DeepSeek-R1（拥有 6710 亿个参数）的知识「浓缩」或「蒸馏」进了参数量小很多的 Qwen-14B （只有 140 亿个参数）之中。

1 The authors curated 24,000 coding problems from TACO Verified, SYNTHETIC-1，and LiveCodeBench). They removed duplicates，problems with less than five unit tests，problems whose solutions failed to pass all associated unit tests，and those that appeared in both test and training sets.

作者从 TACO Verified、SYNTHETIC-1 和 LiveCodeBench 中筛选了 24,000 个编程问题。他们删除了重复的问题，单元测试少于五个的问题，解决方案未能通过所有相关单元测试的问题，以及同时出现在测试集和训练集中的问题。

2 They fine-tuned DeepSeek-R1-Distilled-Qwen-14B using a streamlined reinforcement learning approach that enhancedGroup Relative Policy Optimization (GPRO）with training optimizations from Decoupled Clip and Dynamic Sampling Policy Optimization (DAPO). Among other optimizations，they（i) removed the KL loss（typically used to keep the new model's outputs from straying too far from the base model's outputs），which eliminated the need to compute the base model's output at each training step，and（ii）ignored the loss for outputs that exceeded the output size limit（16,000 tokens for the first training phase，32,000 tokens for the second），which kept the model from being penalized for generating programs that didn't work properly because they had been truncated.

他们采用了一种精简的强化学习方法来微调 DeepSeek-R1-Distilled-Qwen-14B 模型。这种方法在 Group Relative Policy Optimization（GPRO）的基础上进行了增强，并融入了 Decoupled Clip and Dynamic Sampling Policy Optimization（DAPO）中的训练优化技术。在众多优化措施中，他们主要做了两点：其一，移除了 KL 损失。KL 损失通常用于确保新模型的输出不会与基础模型的输出相差太远，移除它就不再需要在每个训练步骤都计算基础模型的输出了。其二，对于超出预设输出大小限制（第一阶段训练限制在 16,000 token，第二阶段为 32,000 token）的输出，模型会忽略其对应的损失。这样做是为了避免模型因为生成了由于被截断而无法正常运行的程序而受到「惩罚」。

3 The authors updated the reinforcement learning library verl to improve the way the model parallelized sampling，computing the reward，and training. Instead of alternating between sampling new outputs，computing rewards，and training（as verl does），they sampled new outputs while training on the previous batch.（They computed the reward immediately after sampling a new output.）For coding problems，this cut total training time in half.

作者更新了强化学习库 verl，改进了模型并行化采样、计算奖励和训练的方式。verl 的做法是轮流进行采样新的输出、计算奖励和训练，而作者的方法是：在训练上一批次数据时，同时进行新输出的采样。（他们在采样新的输出后会立即计算奖励。）对于编码问题，这种方法将总训练时间缩短了一半。

4 To prevent the model from developing behaviors based on flaws in the reward model，the reward model dispensed rewards only when DeepCoder-14B-Preview's output passed all 15 of a problem's most challenging unit tests（judged by input length）within 6 to 12 seconds. Otherwise，the model received no reward.

为了防止模型基于奖励模型中的缺陷而产生行为，奖励模型仅在 DeepCoder-14B-Preview 的输出通过了某个问题所有 15 个最具挑战性的单元测试（根据输入长度判断），并且在 6 到 12 秒内完成时，才发放奖励。否则，模型将不获得奖励。

Results: DeepCoder-14B-Preview is competitive on several coding benchmarks with DeepSeek-R1 as well as proprietary models including OpenAI o3-mini and OpenAI o1，which is believed to be much larger.

结果：DeepCoder-14B-Preview 在多个编码基准上与 DeepSeek-R1 以及 OpenAI o3-mini 和 OpenAI o1 等专有模型具有竞争力，而这些专有模型被认为要大得多。

1 On LiveCodeBench（regularly updated coding problems），DeepCoder-14B-Preview（60.6 percent Pass@1 accuracy）was just shy of o3-mini-2025-1-31 set to low effort（60.9 percent）and slightly ahead of o1-2024-12-17 set to low effort（59.5 percent).

在 LiveCodeBench（一个定期更新的编程问题排行榜）上，DeepCoder-14B-Preview 在 Pass@1 准确率方面达到了 60.6%。这个成绩与设置为低等难度的 o3-mini-2025-1-31 （60.9%）就差一点点，并且略高于设置为低等难度的 o1-2024-12-17 （59.5%）。

2 On Codeforces（competitive coding problems），DeepCoder-14B-Preview（1936 CodeElo，higher is better）performed significantly better than DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-14B（1791 CodeElo). It performed comparably to o3-mini-2025-1-31 set to low effort（1918 CodeElo），o1-2024-12-17 set to low effort（1991 CodeElo），and Deepseek-R1（1948 CodeElo).

在 Codeforces （编程竞赛问题）上，DeepCoder-14B-Preview （1936 CodeElo，越高越好）的表现明显优于 DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-14B （1791 CodeElo）。它的表现与设置为低努力模式的 o3-mini-2025-1-31 （1918 CodeElo）、设置为低努力模式的 o1-2024-12-17 （1991 CodeElo）以及 Deepseek-R1 （1948 CodeElo）相当。

Why it matters: Applying reinforcement learning to coding works，but it has two big issues：(i）Training examples of verifiable code are relatively scarce and（ii）computing reward signals for code is time-consuming，since it requires evaluating many test cases. DeepCoder-14B-Preview's optimizations reduced this complexity，shrinking RL training from months to weeks. Those optimizations are built into Verl-pipeline, an open source RL library from Together.AI and Agentica，giving developers a powerful tool for model training.

为什么它很重要：将强化学习（Reinforcement Learning，RL）应用于编写代码是有效的，但存在两个主要问题：(i）能用来训练的可验证代码样本相对稀缺，以及（ii）计算代码的奖励信号（即判断代码好坏的反馈）非常耗时，因为这需要运行大量测试用例来评估代码。DeepCoder-14B-Preview 通过一系列优化降低了这种复杂性，将强化学习模型的训练时间从数月缩短到了数周。这些优化都整合进了 Verl-pipeline 中，这是一个由 Together.AI 和 Agentica 开发的开源强化学习模型训练库，为开发者提供了强大的工具。

We're thinking: Kudos to the DeepCoder team for open sourcing their reasoning recipe! A handful of companies have developed the know-how to execute RL well，but many teams still have trouble implementing successfully. Open recipes for RL training methods and data curation techniques are important to move the field forward.

我们在思考： 向 DeepCoder 团队致敬，他们开源了他们的推理秘诀！虽然一些公司已经掌握了很好地执行 RL 的专业知识，但许多团队仍然难以成功实施。开放 RL 训练方法和数据整理技术对于推动该领域向前发展非常重要。

#### Memory Layers for More-Factual Output

内存层：让输出更符合事实

Improving a large language model's factual accuracy typically requires making it bigger，which in turn，involves more computation. Researchers devised an architecture that enables models to recall relevant details without significantly increasing the amount of computation required.

提升大语言模型（Large Language Model）的事实准确性（factual accuracy）通常需要让模型变得更大，而模型变大又意味着需要更多的计算资源。研究人员设计了一种新的架构，可以让模型回忆起相关的细节，同时又不会显著增加所需的计算量。

[[2412.09764] Memory Layers at Scale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.09764?utm_campaign=The%20Batch&utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz--g7c5nbOAxyaHzLBSfH2SvzU56mKyBABM33ldUGOUEaGnFyl-_B3i2TWTgENgoPJkhXzaw)

What's new: Vincent-Pierre Berges，Barlas Oğuz，and colleagues at Meta augmented transformers with trainable memory layers that efficiently store and retrieve information related to a prompt. The training code is available under a CC BY-NC license，which permits noncommercial uses.

最新进展：Vincent-Pierre Berges、Barlas Oğuz 以及 Meta 的同事们在 Transformer 模型中加入了一种可训练的记忆层，这种记忆层可以高效地存储和检索与提示相关的信息。训练代码已在 CC BY-NC 许可下发布，该许可允许非商业用途。

Memory layer basics: Memory layers were introduced in 2015 and were applied to transformers a few years later. They compute vectors，which may capture details like names or dates that were learned through training，and retrieve them according to a given input. Computing the output of a memory layer is similar to computing that of a self-attention layer. Both describe vectors that represent queries，keys，and values，and both compute the similarity between queries and keys and then weight the values by that similarity. However，while a self-attention layer computes queries，keys，and values from linear transformations of the input，a memory layer（which computes queries the same way）learns keys and a corresponding value for each key through training.

记忆层基础：记忆层于 2015 年引入，几年后应用于 Transformer。它们计算并存储向量，这些向量可能捕获通过训练学到的细节，例如姓名或日期，并根据给定的输入进行检索。计算记忆层的输出类似于计算自注意力层的输出。两者都使用表示查询、键和值的向量，并且两者都计算查询和键之间的相似度，然后根据该相似度对值进行加权。然而，自注意力层从输入的线性变换计算查询、键和值，而记忆层（它以相同的方式计算查询）通过训练学习键以及每个键对应的值（value）。

Key insight: Memory layers can be scaled to millions of keys，but computing the similarity between a query and so many keys is computationally expensive. One solution is to represent each key as a combination of two half-keys drawn from two much smaller sets. For example，two sets of 1,000 half-keys each can represent 1 million possible keys. Comparing a query to these smaller sets is much more efficient，making it practical to scale up memory layers dramatically.

关键洞察：内存层可以存储数百万个「键」，但要计算一个「查询」和这么多键之间的相似度，计算量会非常大。一种巧妙的解决方案是，将每个键看作是由两个「半键」组合而成，而这两个半键分别来自于两个规模小得多的集合。举个例子，如果两个集合都包含 1000 个半键，它们就能组合出 100 万个不同的键。将查询与这两个小集合进行比较，效率会高很多，这样一来，大幅增加内存层的大小就变得非常实用了。

How it works: The authors pretrained Llama-style models of several sizes（from 134 million to 8 billion parameters）on data similar to Llama 2's and Llama 3's pretraining datasets. They replaced the fully connected layers with memory layers in three transformer blocks. These layers shared parameters and held up to 16 million values（an extra 64 billion parameters total). The memory layers performed these steps:

工作原理：作者使用类似于 Llama 2 和 Llama 3 的预训练数据集，预训练了不同规模的 Llama 模型（参数量从 1.34 亿到 80 亿不等）。他们在三个 Transformer 模块中，用记忆层取代了全连接层。这些记忆层共享参数，并可以存储多达 1600 万个数值（总共额外增加了 640 亿个参数）。记忆层执行了以下步骤：

1 Given a query（a prompt that has been embedded by preceding transformer layers），split it into two vectors half the size.

给定一个查询（由前面的 Transformer 层处理并嵌入的提示），将其分成两个大小减半的向量。

2 Compute similarity scores between each half-query to and each half-key drawn from two sets of half keys. Identify the k highest-scoring half-keys.

计算每个半查询与从两组半键中提取的每个半键之间的相似度得分。找出得分最高的 k 个半键。

3 Concatenate the highest-scoring half keys to produce k2 full keys.

我们将得分最高的「半键」组合起来，生成 k2 个「完整键」。

4 Sum the similarity scores of the two half keys that make up each full key. Choose the k highest-scoring full keys.

接着，我们将构成每个完整键的两个半键的相似性得分相加，并从中挑选出得分最高的 k 个完整键。

5 Compute the index of each full key based on the indices of the corresponding half-keys.

基于对应半键的索引，计算出每个全键的索引。

6 Retrieve the values that correspond to the full keys.

检索与这些全键对应的值。

7 Output the summed values weighted by the similarity scores.

根据相似度分数对数值进行加权求和后输出。

Results: The authors compared a model（8 billion parameters）with memory layers to a similar model without memory layers，both trained on 1 trillion tokens.

结果：作者将一个带有「记忆层」（Memory Layers）的 80 亿参数模型，与一个不带记忆层的类似模型进行了比较。这两个模型都使用了 1 万亿个 Token 的数据进行训练。

1 They used nine question-answering datasets for evaluation. The model with memory layers achieved higher performance on seven of them. For example，on MMLU，the memory model achieved 63.04 percent accuracy，while the unmodified transformer achieved 59.68 percent accuracy.

他们使用了九个问答数据集进行评估。带有记忆层的模型在其中七个数据集上性能更高。例如，在 MMLU 上，记忆模型达到了 63.04％的准确率，而未采用记忆机制的 Transformer 达到了 59.68％的准确率。

2 In general，the memory model performed worse than Llama 3.1 8B trained on 15 trillion tokens. For example，Llama 3.1 8B achieved 66 percent accuracy on MMLU.

总的来说，记忆模型的表现不如在 15 万亿 token 上训练的 Llama 3.1 8B。例如，Llama 3.1 8B 在 MMLU 上取得了 66% 的准确率。

Why it matters: Memory layers didn't catch on in the early days of large language models（LLMs），but they can improve the output of today's much bigger models. LLMs outfitted with memory layers require less data and computation for pretraining than conventional models to achieve the same result，at least with respect to answering factual questions.

为什么这很重要：在大型语言模型（LLMs）的早期，记忆层并未流行起来，但它们可以提升如今规模更大的模型的输出效果。配备记忆层的 LLMs，与传统模型相比，在预训练阶段所需的数据和计算量更少，就可以达到相同的效果，至少在回答事实性问题方面是这样。

We're thinking: While retrieval-augmented generation can help LLMs deliver more-factual output by retrieving facts from a database，the authors add trainable parameters for this purpose.

值得思考的是：尽管检索增强生成（retrieval-augmented generation）能够通过从数据库中检索事实来帮助大语言模型提供更准确的输出，但作者为了实现这一目标，额外增加了可训练的参数。
