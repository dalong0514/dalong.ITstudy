#### Next-Level DeepSeek-R1

下一个层级的 DeepSeek-R1

DeepSeek updated its groundbreaking DeepSeek-R1 large language model to strike another blow for open-weights performance.

DeepSeek 更新了其具有突破性的 DeepSeek-R1 大语言模型，再次提升了开源模型的性能表现。

What's new: The new DeepSeek-R1-0528 surpasses its predecessor and approaches the performance of OpenAI o3 and Google Gemini-2.5 Pro. A smaller version, DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B，runs on a single GPU with as little as 40GB VRAM，according to TechCrunch.

最新进展：新发布的 DeepSeek-R1-0528 模型超越了它的前代产品，性能逼近 OpenAI 的 o3 模型和 Google 的 Gemini-2.5 Pro 模型。根据 TechCrunch 报道，还有一个更小巧的版本 DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B，只需要 40GB 的显存（VRAM），就可以在单个 GPU 上运行。

1 Input/output: Text in（up to 64,000 tokens），text out（up to 64,000 tokens)

输入 / 输出：文本输入（最多 64,000 token），文本输出（最多 64,000 token)

2 Architecture: DeepSeek-R1-0528 mixture-of-experts transformer，685 billion parameters（upgraded from 671 billion），37 billion active at any given time; DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B transformer

架构：DeepSeek-R1-0528 混合专家 Transformer（Transformer），6850 亿参数（从 6710 亿升级），任意时刻激活 370 亿；DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B Transformer

3 Features: JSON output，tool use

功能：JSON 输出，工具使用

4 Availability/price: Both models free via Hugging Face for noncommercial and commercial uses under MIT License，DeepSeek-R1-0528 available via DeepSeek's app by entering the conversation interface and turning on Deep Thinking, DeepSeek API $0.14/$2.19 per 1 million tokens of input/output（$0.035/$0.55 per 1 million tokens of input/output from 4:30 P.M. to 12:30 A.M. Pacific Time)

可用性 / 价格：两种模型都通过 Hugging Face 免费提供，用于非商业和商业用途，遵循 MIT 许可证。DeepSeek-R1-0528 可通过 DeepSeek 的应用程序获得，进入对话界面并打开深度思考。DeepSeek API 输入 / 输出每 100 万 Token 分别为 $0.14/$2.19 （太平洋时间下午 4:30 至凌晨 12:30 输入 / 输出每 100 万 Token 分别为 $0.035/$0.55）。

未公开：微调数据和方法

How it works: DeepSeek released little information so far about how it built the new models.

工作原理：DeepSeek 至今尚未公布关于其如何构建新模型的更多细节。

1 Like the original DeepSeek-R1，DeepSeek-R1-0528 is a fine-tuned version of DeepSeek-V3 from late 2024. It was exposed to further「algorithmic optimization mechanisms during post-training」and consumes more tokens at inference.

就像最初的 DeepSeek-R1 一样，DeepSeek-R1-0528 是 2024 年末 DeepSeek-V3 的一个微调版本。它在后期训练中采用了进一步的算法优化机制，并在推理时需要处理更多的 token。

2 DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B is based on Qwen3-8B with reasoning knowledge distilled from DeepSeek-R1-0528.

DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B 是基于 Qwen3-8B 构建的，并融入了从 DeepSeek-R1-0528 模型中提炼出的推理能力。

Performance: DeepSeek-R1-0528 nips at the heels of top closed LLMs on a variety of benchmarks，while DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B raises the bar for LLMs in its 8-billion-parameter size class. DeepSeek claims general improvements in reasoning，managing complex tasks，and writing and editing lengthy prose，along with 50 percent fewer hallucinations when rewriting and summarizing.

性能：DeepSeek-R1-0528 在多项基准测试中表现出色，紧追顶尖的闭源大语言模型（LLMs)；而 DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B 则将其在 80 亿参数量级别大语言模型的性能推向了新的高度。DeepSeek 宣称该模型在推理能力、处理复杂任务以及撰写和编辑长文本方面都有显著提升，并且在进行文本重写和总结时，模型出现「幻觉」（即生成不实信息）的概率降低了 50%。

1 DeepSeek-R1-0528 improves on the previous version dramatically in some cases. In DeepSeek's tests，it achieved 17.7 percent of the reasoning problems in HLE compared to the previous version's 8.5 percent. On Aider，it achieved 71.6 percent accuracy compared to the previous version's 53.3 percent accuracy，and it made a similar improvement on AIME 2025（math）— although it consumed nearly twice as many tokens.

DeepSeek-R1-0528 在某些测试中比之前的版本有了大幅提升。在 DeepSeek 的测试中，它在 HLE 的推理问题上解决率达到了 17.7%，而之前的版本只有 8.5%。在 Aider 上，它的准确率从之前版本的 53.3% 提升到了 71.6%，并且在 AIME 2025（数学）上也取得了类似的进步 —— 不过，完成同样的任务它消耗的 token 数量几乎是之前的两倍。

2 On AIME 2024 and AIME 2025（high-school math competition problems）as well as LiveCodeBench (coding challenges），DeepSeek-R1-0528 performed ahead of Gemini-2.5 Pro-0506 but behind o3. On GPQA Diamond（graduate-level knowledge in a variety of domains），Aider（programming tasks），and HLE（reasoning），it fell behind both Gemini-2.5 Pro-0506 and o3.

在 AIME 2024 和 AIME 2025（高中数学竞赛问题）以及 LiveCodeBench（编程挑战）上，DeepSeek-R1-0528 的表现优于 Gemini-2.5 Pro-0506，但落后于 o3。在 GPQA Diamond（跨领域的研究生水平知识）、Aider（编程任务）和 HLE（推理）上，它落后于 Gemini-2.5 Pro-0506 和 o3。

3 DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B excelled on AIME 2025，where it achieved 76.3 percent，ahead of the much larger Qwen3-32B（72.9 percent）and just behind o3-mini set to medium effort（76.7 percent). It did less well on GPQA，underperforming the other models reported by DeepSeek，and LiveCodeBench，where it fell behind Gemini 2.5-Flash-Thinking-0520.

DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B 在 AIME 2025 竞赛中表现出色，取得了 76.3% 的好成绩，领先于规模大得多的 Qwen3-32B 模型（72.9%），仅略微落后于设置为中等难度的 o3-mini（76.7%）。然而，它在 GPQA 测试中的表现相对较差，不如 DeepSeek 报告的其他模型；在 LiveCodeBench 代码基准测试中，它也落后于 Gemini 2.5-Flash-Thinking-0520 模型。

Behind the news: The initial version of DeepSeek-R1 challenged the belief that building top-performing AI models requires tens to hundreds of millions of dollars，top-of-the-line GPUs，and enormous numbers of GPU hours. For the second time in less than a year，DeepSeek has built a competitive LLM with a relatively low budget.

新闻背后：DeepSeek-R1 的初始版本挑战了人们普遍认为的观点，即构建性能卓越的 AI 模型需要投入数千万到数亿美元、使用顶级的 GPU 和耗费巨大的 GPU 计算资源。DeepSeek 在不到一年的时间里，第二次以相对较低的预算构建出了具有竞争力的大语言模型（LLM）。

Why it matters: DeepSeek's models，along with Alibaba's Qwen series，continue to narrow the gap between open-weights models and their closed peers. Its accomplishments could lead to wider adoption of less-expensive，more-efficient approaches. DeepSeek is passing along the cost savings to developers，offering high-performance inference at a fraction of the cost of closed models.

重要性：DeepSeek 的模型，连同阿里巴巴的 Qwen 系列，正在持续缩小开源模型与闭源模型之间的差距。DeepSeek 的成就可能会促使大家更广泛地采用成本更低、效率更高的方法。DeepSeek 正在把节省下来的成本让利给开发者，以远低于闭源模型的成本提供高性能的推理服务。

We're thinking: DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B mixes contributions from open-weight models — possible only because Qwen3's license，like DeepSeek's is permissive. Open models enable experimentation and innovation in ways that closed models do not.

我们认为，DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B 整合了开源模型的优点。这之所以能实现，是因为 Qwen3 的许可协议和 DeepSeek 一样，都是宽松的。与闭源模型不同，开源模型能够以更多样的方式促进实验和创新。

#### Machine Translation in Action

机器翻译在行动

AI is bringing a massive boost in productivity to Duolingo，maker of the most popular app for learning languages.

AI 正在给 Duolingo 带来生产力上的巨大提升，它开发了最受欢迎的语言学习应用。

What's new: Duolingo used generative AI to produce 148 courses，more than doubling its previous catalog. The technology enabled the company to offer some of its most popular courses — Spanish，French，German，Italian，Japanese，Korean，and Mandarin — in 28 languages. Initially，the company is using AI to produce courses aimed at beginners，with more advanced levels to come.

新动态：Duolingo 利用生成式 AI （Generative AI）制作了 148 门课程，这使其课程数量比之前翻了一倍多。这项技术使得该公司能够用 28 种不同的语言提供其最受欢迎的一些课程，包括西班牙语、法语、德语、意大利语、日语、韩语和普通话。最初，Duolingo 主要使用 AI 开发针对初学者的课程，未来将陆续推出更高级别的课程。

How it works: Duolingo's AI-assisted approach to building language courses quickly turns a single course into many. The new approach revved its pace from building 100 courses over 12 years to producing many more than that in less than a year.

工作原理：多邻国利用 AI 辅助的方式构建语言课程，这种方法能快速地将一门课程拓展成多门课程。这种新方法极大地提升了速度，从过去 12 年构建 100 门课程，到现在不到一年就能产出远超这个数量的课程。

1 Duolingo starts by building a base course and uses AI to translate it into numerous languages. For example，it can adapt a course that enables English speakers to learn French into a course for Mandarin speakers.

多邻国（Duolingo）首先构建基础课程，然后使用 AI 将其翻译成多种语言。例如，它可以将原本为英语用户设计的法语课程，改编成适合普通话用户的课程。

2 The new process gives the company more flexibility in allocating resources，Duolingo's head of AI Klinton Bicknell told Bloomberg. Previously，the company could dedicate a team to either creating new high-demand courses or updating an existing course. Now it can do both.

Duolingo 的 AI 负责人 Klinton Bicknell 告诉彭博社，新的流程让公司在资源分配上更具灵活性。之前，公司只能将一个团队专注于创建新的高需求课程或更新现有课程。现在，他们可以两者兼顾了。

3 The quicker pace will enable the company to meet rising demand for instruction in Asian languages such as Japanese，Korean，and Mandarin.

加快的速度将有助于 Duolingo 满足日益增长的亚洲语言教学需求，例如日语、韩语和普通话。

Behind the scenes: AI is at the heart of Duolingo's expansion into other areas beyond language learning.

背后的故事：AI 是 Duolingo 将业务扩展到语言学习之外的核心驱动力。

1 Duolingo has used OpenAI models to build curricula since 2023. However，it is evaluating models from Anthropic and Google as well as open options.

Duolingo 自 2023 年起便开始利用 OpenAI 的模型构建其课程体系。不过，目前他们也在对 Anthropic、Google 的模型以及其他的开源方案进行评估。

Bicknell 透露，在一项测试后，Duolingo 认为 Anthropic 的 Claude 在为公司相对较新的数学课程生成某些类型的数学内容方面表现「远胜一筹」。

上周，该公司因采纳 AI 而受到批评，此前 CEO Luis von Ahn 最近在 LinkedIn 上发文表示，公司将停止雇用承包商来处理可自动化的工作，并仅在无法自动化的领域增加人员。此后，Duolingo 澄清表示，计划雇用更多工程师和 AI 研究人员，并且员工将负责生成用于训练 AI 的数据，而不是执行质量审查和其他可由 AI 更快完成的工作。

#### AI Uses Energy，AI Saves Energy

人工智能消耗能源，也节省能源

AI's thirst for energy is growing，but the technology also could help produce huge energy savings over the next five to 10 years，according to a recent report.

AI 的能源需求正在快速增长，但根据最近的一份报告，这项技术在未来五到十年内也有望带来巨大的能源节约。

What's new: The International Energy Agency（IEA），which advises 44 countries on energy policy，performed a comprehensive analysis of AI's energy consumption including energy required to obtain critical materials needed for chips and data centers. The report sees dark clouds ahead but also silver linings.

最新消息：国际能源署（IEA）是一家为 44 个国家提供能源政策建议的机构，他们对 AI 的能源消耗进行了全面分析，其中包括获取芯片和数据中心所需关键材料所需的能源。报告指出，虽然挑战重重，但也蕴藏着机遇。

Dark clouds: The report，which is based on interviews with officials in government，energy，and technology，makes four projections for AI's energy consumption. In the base scenario，future growth and efficiency gains are similar to those of the past five years. The agency also plots a「take-off」scenario in which AI adoption happens faster，a「high efficiency」scenario with lower energy needs，and a「headwinds」scenario in which adoption of AI slows or infrastructure bottlenecks impede construction. Among the conclusions:

乌云密布：这份报告基于对政府、能源和技术领域官员的采访，对 AI 的能源消耗提出了四个预测。在基础情景下，未来的增长和效率提升与过去五年相似。该机构还设想了「起飞」情景，其中 AI 的采用速度更快；「高效率」情景，能源需求较低；以及「逆风」情景，其中 AI 的采用放缓或基础设施瓶颈阻碍建设。得出的结论包括：

1 Demand for electricity by data centers worldwide will more than double by 2030 in the base scenario，growing from 415 terawatt-hours（TWh）today to 945 TWh，around 2.5 percent of current global energy consumption. By 2035，this figure will range from 700 TWh to 1700 TWh.

在基本情景下，到 2030 年，全球数据中心的电力需求将增加一倍以上，从目前的 415 太瓦时（TWh）增长到 945 太瓦时，大约占当前全球能源消耗总量的 2.5%。到 2035 年，这一数字预计将在 700 太瓦时到 1700 太瓦时之间。

2 By 2030，data centers outfitted with AI accelerator chips will consume four times the energy they do today.

到 2030 年，配备了人工智能（AI）加速芯片的数据中心，其能源消耗量将达到如今的四倍。

3 The United States，China，and Europe have more data centers（and use more electricity）than the rest of the world. Like many countries，their data centers are in a few geographic regions，drawing from the same power sources，which eventually will strain local electrical grids. Together，the U.S. and China will account for 80 percent of global growth in data center electricity consumption by 2030. Japan and Malaysia will also see strong growth.

与世界其他地区相比，美国、中国和欧洲拥有数量更多的数据中心（以及更高的用电量）。与许多国家类似，它们的数据中心集中在少数几个地理区域，共用同一电源，这最终会给当地电网带来巨大压力。到 2030 年，全球数据中心电力消耗的增长量中，将有 80% 来自美国和中国。日本和马来西亚的数据中心电力消耗也将显著增长。

Silver linings: AI already makes energy generation，distribution，and use more efficient. The authors expect these savings to accelerate.

好消息是，AI 已经让能源的产生、分配和使用更加高效。作者们预计，这些节省将加速实现。

1 Existing AI algorithms predict energy generation and consumption. This makes it easier to integrate renewable energy sources into the grid，which reduces reliance on fossil fuels and cuts the resulting pollutants and greenhouse gases. Extending existing programs to increase use of renewables by 1 percent would reduce CO2 emissions by 120 megatons by 2035，which is roughly 40 percent of the projected emissions attributable to data centers.

现有的 AI 算法可以预测能源的产生和消耗。这使得可再生能源更容易并入电网，从而减少对化石燃料的依赖，并减少由此产生的污染物和温室气体。将现有项目推广开来，如果能将可再生能源的使用量提高 1%，到 2035 年将减少 1.2 亿吨二氧化碳排放，这大约占数据中心预计排放量的 40%。

2 Widespread adoption of existing AI applications that streamline energy consumption in industry，transportation，and buildings could reduce CO2 emissions by 1.4 gigatons，nearly five times the projected emissions attributable to data centers，by 2035. For example，scaling up existing AI optimization of heating，ventilation，and air-conditioning systems would save 300 TWh，about one-third of total energy used by data centers.

广泛采用现有的 AI 应用，这些应用能够优化工业、交通和建筑中的能源消耗，到 2035 年，可将二氧化碳排放量减少 1.4 千兆吨。这几乎是数据中心预计排放量的五倍。例如，扩大现有 AI 对供暖、通风和空调系统的优化，将节省 300 TWh 的能源，这约占数据中心总能耗的三分之一。

3 AI and cloud-computing companies continue to negotiate long-term purchase agreements that can secure renewable and zero-emissions energy for as much as 20 years. Data center operators are responsible for most of the long-term contracts that have been announced，nearly all of them for solar energy. Consequently，renewables generation is projected to grow by over 450 TWh by 2035.

AI 和云计算公司继续谈判长期采购协议，这些协议可以确保长达 20 年的可再生和零排放能源供应。已宣布的大部分长期合同都由数据中心运营商签署，其中几乎所有合同都涉及太阳能。因此，到 2035 年，可再生能源发电量预计将增长超过 450 TWh。

4 The energy costs of training，inference，and cooling hardware are expected to fall further thanks to trends in AI models（fewer parameters，more efficient algorithms，task-specific models）hardware（more energy-efficient chips，improved cooling methods），and usage（batch processing，running smaller models locally rather than in the cloud).

得益于 AI 模型在参数减少、算法效率提升以及特定任务模型方面的进步，硬件在更节能芯片和改进冷却方法方面的优化，以及使用方式在批处理和本地运行小型模型（而非依赖云端）上的改变，训练、推理和冷却硬件的能源成本预计会进一步降低。

Yes，but: The authors concede that lower energy costs for AI likely will lead to much greater consumption — according to the Jevons paradox — so more-efficient models and hardware will result in higher energy consumption overall.

是的，但是：作者承认，尽管 AI 的能源成本可能会降低，但这很可能导致能源消耗大幅增加。根据 Jevons 悖论，更高效的模型和硬件反而会带来更高的总体能源消耗。

Behind the news: Data centers were growing rapidly prior to the boom in generative AI. Data centers' electricity use doubled between 2000 and 2005 and again between 2017 and 2022，driven by the growth of cloud computing and data storage，streaming and social media，and cryptocurrency mining. However，these periods of accelerating growth were followed by periods of slower growth as efforts to cut costs led to more-efficient software and hardware. The authors expect this pattern to hold.

新闻背景：在生成式 AI （Generative AI）爆发之前，数据中心早已呈现快速增长的态势。得益于云计算和数据存储、流媒体和社交媒体以及加密货币挖矿的兴起，数据中心的用电量在 2000 年至 2005 年期间翻了一番，并在 2017 年至 2022 年期间再次翻番。然而，在这些加速增长的时期之后，增速有所放缓，这主要是因为降本增效的努力推动了软件和硬件效率的提升。作者们预计这种增长模式将会延续。

Why it matters: The IEA report is a first-of-its-kind analysis of AI's energy requirements，how they're likely to grow，as well as the potential of the technology itself to reduce those requirements. It confirms that AI is poised to consume huge amounts of energy. However，it also suggests that today's energy costs will be tomorrow's energy savings as AI makes energy generation，distribution，and use more efficient across a wide variety of industries.

这项研究为何重要：国际能源署（IEA）的这份报告，首次对人工智能（AI）的能源需求、未来的增长趋势，以及这项技术本身降低能源消耗的潜力进行了深入分析。报告证实，AI 有望消耗巨大的能源。然而，报告也指出，随着 AI 在各行各业提高能源生产、分配和使用的效率，今天的能源投入将转化为未来的能源节约。

We're thinking: While demand for electricity for data centers is growing rapidly, calibrating the right level of investment is tricky. High levels of growth come with high levels of hype that can lead analysts to overestimate future demand. For example，Microsoft，after examining its forecasts, canceled data-center projects that would have consumed 2 gigawatts.

我们在思考：虽然数据中心的电力需求正在快速增长，但确定合适的投资水平是很棘手的。高增长伴随着大量的炒作，这可能导致分析师高估未来需求。例如，Microsoft 在检查其预测后，取消了消耗 2 千兆瓦的数据中心项目。

#### Phishing for Agents

AI 智能体面临「钓鱼」攻击

Researchers identified a simple way to mislead autonomous agents based on large language models.

研究人员发现了一种简单的方法来误导基于大语言模型的自主 AI 智能体。

What's new：Ang Li and colleagues at Columbia University developed a method to exploit the implicit trust that agents tend to place in popular websites by poisoning those websites with malicious links.

新闻：哥伦比亚大学的 Ang Li 及其同事开发了一种新方法，他们利用 AI 智能体往往会对流行网站产生隐式信任的特点，通过在这些网站中植入恶意链接来进行攻击。

Key insight: Commercially available agentic systems may not trust random sites on the web，but they tend to trust popular sites such as social-media sites. An attacker can exploit this trust by crafting seemingly typical posts that link to a malicious website. The agent might follow the link，mistakenly extending its trust to an untrustworthy site.

关键洞察：市面上可用的 AI 智能体（AI Agent）系统可能不信任网络上的随机网站，但它们倾向于信任流行的网站，例如社交媒体网站。攻击者可以利用这种信任，制作看似典型的帖子，这些帖子链接到恶意网站。AI 智能体可能会点击链接，错误地将它的信任赋予了一个不可信的网站。

How it works：The authors tested web-browsing agents including Anthropic Computer Use and MultiOn on tasks such as shopping or sending emails.

How it works：作者测试了包括 Anthropic Computer Use 和 MultiOn 在内的网页浏览 AI 智能体（AI agent），执行诸如购物或发送电子邮件等任务。

1 The authors created Reddit posts that aligned thematically with a particular agentic task，such as shopping for Air Jordan 1 shoes. The posts contained text akin to marketing（for example,「Where to Buy Air Jordan 1 Chicago」）as well as instructions that pointed to a malicious site controlled by the authors（「for more information，check out <website>」).

作者创建了主题与特定 AI 智能体任务相符的 Reddit 帖子，例如购买 Air Jordan 1 鞋。这些帖子包含带有营销性质的文本（例如，「哪里可以买到 Air Jordan 1 Chicago」），以及包含指向作者控制的恶意网站的说明（「欲了解更多信息，请访问 <website>」）。

2 The authors fed a query like「Where can I buy Nike Air Jordan 1 in Chicago?」to the agent. They also entered sensitive information like credit card details or email credentials.

作者向智能体输入了「在芝加哥哪里可以买到 Nike Air Jordan 1？」这样的查询。他们还输入了敏感信息，比如信用卡详细信息或电子邮件凭据。

3 The agent searched the web for resources needed to fulfill the query. It examined sites and found the Reddit posts written by the authors.

该代理在网络上搜索了完成查询所需的资源。它检查了网站，并找到了作者撰写的 Reddit 帖子。

4 The agent followed the instructions in the posts and visited the malicious website. The website included instructions that manipulated the agent to pursue an attacker's goal，such as submitting credit card information or sending phishing emails from the user's email address.

该代理按照帖子中的说明访问了恶意网站。该网站包含的指令可以操纵代理，使其去实现攻击者的目标，例如提交信用卡信息或从用户的电子邮件地址发送网络钓鱼电子邮件。

Results：Once an agent was redirected to the malicious websites，it reliably followed the attacker's instructions. For example，each of the agents tested divulged credit card information in 10 out of 10 trials. Similarly，each agent sent a phishing message from the user's email account asking recipients to send money to a malicious「friend」in 10 out of 10 trials.

结果：一旦 AI 智能体被重定向到恶意网站，它就会可靠地执行攻击者的指令。例如，在测试中，所有 AI 智能体在 10 次尝试中有 10 次都泄露了信用卡信息。同样，所有 AI 智能体在 10 次尝试中有 10 次都从用户的电子邮件账户发送了网络钓鱼邮件，要求收件人向一个伪装成「朋友」的恶意目标汇款。

Why it matters：Giving agents the ability to perform real-world actions，such as executing purchases and sending emails，raises the possibility that they might be tricked into taking harmful actions. Manipulating agents by referring them to malicious web content is an effective vector of attack. Agents will be more secure if they're designed to avoid and resist such manipulation.

为什么这很重要：让 AI 智能体（AI Agent）具备执行现实世界行动的能力，例如完成购买和发送电子邮件，也带来了一个潜在风险：它们可能会被诱骗去执行有害操作。其中一种有效的攻击手段就是通过将 AI 智能体引导至恶意网络内容来对其进行操纵。如果 AI 智能体在设计时就能避免和抵御此类操纵，它们将更加安全。

We're thinking: Humans，too，can be fooled by phishing and other malicious activities，and the path to programming agents to defend against them seems easier than the path to training the majority of humans to do so. In the long term，agents will make online interactions safer.

我们正在思考：人类也会被网络钓鱼和其他恶意活动所欺骗，而通过编程 AI 智能体（AI Agent）来防御这些攻击，似乎比训练绝大多数人去识别和应对这些攻击要容易得多。从长远来看，智能体将让在线交互变得更安全。
