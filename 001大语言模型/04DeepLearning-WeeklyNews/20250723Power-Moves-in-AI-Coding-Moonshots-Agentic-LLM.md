#### Born to Be Agentic

天生就是智能体

An agent's performance depends not only on an effective workflow but also on a large language model that excels at agentic activities. A new open-weights model focuses on those capabilities.

一个 AI 智能体（AI Agent）的表现，不仅取决于其高效的工作流程，更离不开一个擅长执行各类智能体活动的大语言模型（Large Language Model）。为此，一个新的开放权重（open-weights）模型正专注于提升这些关键能力。

What's new: Beijing-based Moonshot AI released the Kimi K2 family of 1 trillion-parameter large language models (LLMs). The family includes the pretrained Kimi-K2-Base and Kimi-K2-Instruct，which is fine-tuned for core agentic tasks，notably tool use. Bucking the recent trend in LLMs，Kimi K2 models are not trained for chain-of-thought reasoning.

最新动态：位于北京的 Moonshot AI 公司发布了 Kimi K2 系列万亿参数大语言模型（LLM）。该系列包括预训练的 Kimi-K2-Base 模型和 Kimi-K2-Instruct 模型，其中 Kimi-K2-Instruct 针对核心 AI 智能体（AI Agent）任务，特别是工具使用能力进行了微调。值得注意的是，Kimi K2 模型并没有像近期其他大语言模型那样，针对思维链（chain-of-thought）推理进行训练。

1 Input/output: Text in（up to around 128,000 tokens），text out (up to around 16,000 tokens)

输入 / 输出：文本输入（支持高达约 128,000 tokens），文本输出（支持高达约 16,000 tokens）

2 Architecture: Mixture-of-experts transformer，1 trillion parameters total，32 billion parameters active

架构：采用专家混合 Transformer（Mixture-of-experts transformer）架构，模型总参数量达到 1 万亿，其中 320 亿参数处于活跃状态。

3 Performance: Outperforms other open-weights，non-reasoning models in tool use，coding，math，and general-knowledge benchmarks

性能：在工具使用、编程、数学和通用知识基准测试中，这款模型超越了其他开源（open-weights）、非推理（non-reasoning）模型。

4 Availability: Web interface (free), API ($0.60/$0.15/$2.50 per million input/cached/output tokens), weights available for non-commercial and commercial uses up to 100 million monthly active users or monthly revenue of $20,000,000 under「modified MIT license」

可用性：它提供网页界面（免费使用），也支持 API （应用程序编程接口）调用，费用为每百万输入 Token （标记）$0.60、每百万缓存 Token $0.15、每百万输出 Token $2.50。此外，其模型权重（weights）可用于非商业和商业用途，但需遵循「修改后的 MIT 许可证」，且每月活跃用户不得超过 1 亿，或月收入不得超过 $20,000,000。

5 Features: Tool use including web search and arbitrary tools

功能：能够使用工具，包括进行网络搜索以及使用多种其他工具。

6 Undisclosed: Specific training methods，training datasets

未披露信息：具体的训练方法和训练数据集。

How it works: Moonshot pretrained the models on 15.5 trillion tokens from undisclosed sources. It fine-tuned Kimi-K2-Instruct via reinforcement learning using a proprietary dataset.

工作原理：Moonshot 公司使用来自未公开来源的 15.5 万亿个 Token （令牌）对模型进行了预训练。随后，它又利用一个专有的数据集，通过强化学习（reinforcement learning）的方式对 Kimi-K2-Instruct 模型进行了微调（fine-tuned）。

1 To enable Kimi-K2-Instruct to use tools，the team generated a large dataset of examples in which models used tools，both real-world and synthetic，that implement model context protocol (MCP). Unidentified models acted as users，and other unidentified models acted as agents that solved tasks assigned by the users.  A further model acted as a judge to filter out unsuccessful examples.

为了让 Kimi-K2-Instruct 能够熟练使用各种工具，团队构建了一个庞大的示例数据集。这些示例中包含了模型如何使用工具的各种场景，这些工具既有来自真实世界的，也有人工合成的，它们都遵循了模型上下文协议（Model Context Protocol，MCP）。具体来说，一部分匿名的模型扮演了用户的角色，而另一些匿名模型则充当了 AI 智能体（AI Agent），负责解决用户提出的各项任务。此外，还有一个模型作为裁判，专门负责筛选并剔除那些未能成功完成任务的案例。

2 The team fine-tuned Kimi-K2-Instruct via reinforcement learning. The model evaluated its own performance，used its evaluation as a reward，and iteratively improved its performance.

该团队通过强化学习（Reinforcement Learning）对 Kimi-K2-Instruct 进行了微调。这个模型会评估自身表现，并将其评估结果作为奖励，在迭代中不断提升自身性能。

3 The team also fine-tuned Kimi-K2-Instruct to solve coding and math problems via reinforcement learning. The model did not evaluate its own performance on these problems; it determined rewards according to pre-existing solutions or unit tests.

该团队还对 Kimi-K2-Instruct 模型进行了微调，使其能够通过强化学习（reinforcement learning）来解决编程和数学难题。值得注意的是，模型在解决这些问题时，并不会自行评估表现；它会根据预先存在的解决方案或单元测试来确定相应的「奖励」。

Results: Moonshot compared Kimi-K2-Instruct to two open-weights，non-reasoning models（DeepSeek-V3 and Qwen3-235B-A22B with reasoning switched off）and four closed，non-reasoning models.

结果：Moonshot 将 Kimi-K2-Instruct 与两类模型进行了比较：一类是两个开放权重（open-weights）且不具备推理能力（non-reasoning）的模型（包括 DeepSeek-V3 和 Qwen3-235B-A22B，它们都关闭了推理功能）；另一类是四个封闭（closed）且不具备推理能力的模型。

1 Kimi-K2-Instruct outperformed the open-weights models across a range of benchmarks for tool use，coding，math，reasoning，and general knowledge.

Kimi-K2-Instruct 在工具使用、编程、数学、推理和通用知识等一系列基准测试中，超越了开源模型。

2 It achieved middling performance relative to the closed models，though it did relatively well in math and science tasks.

尽管与闭源模型相比，它的整体表现处于中等水平，但在数学和科学任务上，Kimi-K2-Instruct 的表现还是相对不错的。

3 Compared to all models tested，on LiveCodeBench (coding tasks），Kimi K2（53 percent）achieved the best performance，ahead of Claude Sonnet 4 with extended thinking mode switched off（48.5 percent).

在所有参评模型中，针对 LiveCodeBench（编码任务）的测试结果显示，Kimi K2（53%）表现最为出色，超过了关闭了扩展思考模式的 Claude Sonnet 4（48.5%）。

4 Among all models tested，on AceBench (tool use），Kimi K2（76.5 percent accuracy）placed second behind GPT 4.1（80.1 percent accuracy).

在所有测试模型中，在 AceBench （工具使用）上，Kimi K2 以 76.5% 的准确率位居第二，仅次于 GPT 4.1（准确率为 80.1% ）。

5 On 8 out of 11 math and science benchmarks，Kimi K2 achieved the best performance of all models tested.

在 11 项数学和科学基准测试中，Kimi K2 在其中 8 项的表现优于所有其他测试模型。

Behind the news: Third-party vendors have been quick to implement Kimi-K2-Instruct.

新闻速递：第三方供应商已迅速采用 Kimi-K2-Instruct。

1 The Groq's platform accelerates Kimi-K2-Instruct's output to about 200 tokens per second（$1/$3 per million input/output tokens）compared to 45 tokens per second reported by Artificial Analysis.

Groq 的平台将 Kimi-K2-Instruct 的输出速度提升至每秒约 200 Token（Token），而每百万输入 Token（Token）的成本为 1 美元，每百万输出 Token 的成本为 3 美元。相比之下，Artificial Analysis 报告的平均速度为每秒 45 Token。

2 The fine-tuning platform Unsloth released quantized versions that run on local devices that have 250 gigabytes of combined hard-disk capacity，RAM，and VRAM.

微调平台 Unsloth 发布了其量化版本，这些版本可以在总计拥有 250 GB 硬盘、内存（RAM）和显存（VRAM）的本地设备上运行。

Why it matters: Demand is growing for LLMs that carry out agentic workflows accurately，as these workflows lead to better performance. Kimi-K2-Instruct gives developers a strong option for fine-tuning models for their own agentic tasks.

重要性：市场对能够准确执行 AI 智能体工作流程（agentic workflows）的大语言模型（LLM）的需求正日益增长，因为这类工作流程能够带来更优异的性能。Kimi-K2-Instruct 为开发者提供了一个强大的选择，帮助他们为自己的 AI 智能体任务微调模型。

We're thinking: Early LLMs were built to generate output for human consumption. But the rise of agentic workflows means that more and more LLM output is consumed by computers，so it makes good sense to put more research and training effort into building LLMs that generate output for computers. A leading LLM optimized for agentic workflows is a boon to developers!

我们认为：早期的大语言模型（LLM）主要是为了生成供人类阅读和理解的内容而设计的。然而，随着 AI 智能体（AI Agent）工作流的兴起，越来越多的大语言模型（LLM）输出需要由计算机来处理。因此，将更多的研究和训练精力投入到构建能够为计算机生成输出的大语言模型（LLM）上，显得非常有意义。一个针对 AI 智能体工作流进行优化的领先大语言模型，将无疑是开发者们的福音！

#### How to Comply With the EU's AI Act

如何遵守欧盟的 AI 法案

The European Union published guidelines to help builders of AI models to comply with the AI Act，which was enacted last year.

欧盟发布了指导方针，旨在帮助人工智能（AI）模型的开发者遵守去年通过并生效的《人工智能法案》（AI Act）。

What's new: The General Purpose AI Code of Practice outlines voluntary procedures to comply with provisions of the AI Act that govern general-purpose models. Companies that follow the guidelines will benefit from simplified compliance，greater legal certainty，and potentially lower administrative costs，according to EU officials. Those that don't must comply with the law nonetheless，which may prove more costly. While Microsoft，Mistral，and OpenAI said they would follow the guidelines，Meta declined，saying that Europe is「heading down the wrong path on AI.」

最新进展：欧盟新出台的《通用人工智能行为准则》（General Purpose AI Code of Practice）概述了一系列自愿遵守程序，旨在帮助企业符合《人工智能法案》（AI Act）中规范通用模型的相关条款。欧盟官员表示，遵循这些准则的公司将享受到合规流程简化、法律确定性增强以及行政成本可能降低的好处。而那些不遵循准则的公司，即便如此也必须遵守法律规定，但这可能会导致更高的合规成本。目前，Microsoft、Mistral 和 OpenAI 都表示将遵守这些准则，但 Meta 却拒绝了，并声称欧洲「在人工智能领域正在走上错误的道路。」

How it works: The code focuses on「general-purpose AI models」that are capable of performing a wide range of tasks.

工作原理：这项规定主要关注能够执行广泛任务的「通用 AI 模型」（general-purpose AI models）。

1 Stricter rules apply to models that are deemed to pose「systemic risk,」or「a risk that is specific to the high-impact capabilities」owing to a model's reach or clear potential for producing negative effects. Managers of such models must perform continuous assessment and mitigation，including identifying and analyzing systemic risks and evaluating how acceptable they are. They must protect against unauthorized access and insider threats.

对于那些被认为可能带来「系统性风险」（systemic risk），或者因其覆盖范围广、存在产生负面影响的明确潜力而导致「特定高影响力能力风险」（high-impact capabilities）的模型，将适用更严格的规则。这类模型的管理者必须持续进行评估和风险应对工作，包括识别和分析系统性风险，并评估这些风险的可接受程度。同时，他们还必须防范未经授权的访问和内部威胁。

2 Developers who build models that pose systemic risk must maintain a variety of documentation. They must disclose training data and sources，how they obtained rights to the data，the resulting model's properties，their testing methods，and computational resources and energy consumed. They must file updates when they make significant changes or upon request of parties that use the model. They must report mishaps and model misbehavior. They must file a report within 2 days of becoming aware of an event that led to a serious and irreversible disruption of critical infrastructure，5 days to report a cybersecurity breach，and 10 days a model's responsibility for a human death.

负责开发和构建可能带来系统性风险（systemic risk）模型（model）的开发者，必须妥善保存各种文档。他们需要披露训练数据（training data）的来源和详情、如何取得数据使用授权、所开发模型的具体属性、测试方法，以及计算资源和能源的消耗情况。当模型发生重大变更时，或应模型使用方的要求，他们必须及时提交更新报告。此外，他们还需报告模型运行中的事故和异常行为。具体来说，在得知导致关键基础设施（critical infrastructure）出现严重且不可逆转中断的事件后，必须在 2 天内提交报告；发现网络安全漏洞（cybersecurity breach）后，需在 5 天内报告；如果模型被认定对人类死亡负有责任，则必须在 10 天内提交报告。

3 The code doesn't mention penalties for noncompliance or violations. Further，the code doesn't discuss the cost of compliance except to say that assessing and mitigating systemic risks「merits significant investment of time and resources.」In 2024，Germany's Federal Statistical Office estimated that the cost of compliance for a high-risk system would come to roughly $600,000 to get started and another $100,000 annually.

该法规并未提及不合规或违规行为将面临何种处罚。此外，除了笼统地指出评估和缓解系统性风险「值得投入大量时间和资源」外，法规也没有详细讨论具体的合规成本。然而，在 2024 年，德国联邦统计局估算，一个高风险系统的合规成本初期投入大约需要 600,000 美元，之后每年还需要约 100,000 美元。

Behind the news: The AI Act is the product of years of debate and lobbying among scores of stakeholders. EU technology official Henna Virkkunen called the AI Act「an important step」in making cutting-edge models「not only innovative but also safe and transparent.」However，companies and governments on both sides of the Atlantic have asserted that the law goes too far. In May，the EU moved to relax some provisions，including language that would allow users to sue AI companies for damages caused by their systems. Earlier this month，44 chief executives at top European companies asked European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen to postpone the AI Act's rules that govern general-purpose models for two years.

新闻背后：AI 法案是经过多年辩论以及众多利益相关者游说（lobbying）的产物。欧盟技术官员 Henna Virkkunen 称，AI 法案是让前沿模型（cutting-edge models）「不仅具有创新性，而且安全透明」的「重要一步」。然而，大西洋两岸的公司和政府都认为，这项法律过于严苛。今年五月，欧盟采取行动放宽了一些规定，包括允许用户起诉 AI 公司因其系统造成的损害而索赔的条款。本月初，44 位欧洲顶尖公司的首席执行官联合致信欧盟委员会（European Commission）主席 Ursula von der Leyen，请求将 AI 法案中针对通用目的模型（general-purpose models）的管理规则推迟两年实施。

Why it matters: The AI Act is the most comprehensive and far-reaching set of AI regulations enacted to date，yet it remains highly contentious and in flux. The commitments by Microsoft，Mistral，and OpenAI to follow the code mark a significant step in the act's circuitous path to implementation，but also an increase in bureaucracy and potential for regulatory capture. Their endorsement could persuade other big companies to sign on and weaken further efforts to loosen the act's requirements.

重要性：《人工智能法案》（AI Act）是迄今为止出台的最全面、影响力最广泛的人工智能（AI）监管法规，但目前它仍备受争议，并且仍在不断调整中。Microsoft、Mistral 和 OpenAI 这三家公司承诺遵守该法案设定的准则，标志着该法案曲折的实施之路上迈出了重要一步。然而，这也可能增加监管的繁琐程度，并提升了「监管俘获」(regulatory capture）的风险。这些巨头的认可，可能会促使其他大型公司也加入进来，从而削弱未来进一步放松该法案要求的尝试。

We're thinking: From a regulatory point of view，the notion of systemic risk is misguided. Limiting the inherent risk of AI models is as helpful as limiting the inherent risk of electric motors，which would result only in relatively useless motors. We hope for further revisions in the AI Act that relieve burdens on builders of foundation models，especially open source projects，and address practical risks of specific applications rather than theoretical risks of their underlying technology.

我们认为：从监管角度来看，谈论「系统性风险」的概念其实存在误区。限制人工智能（AI）模型固有的风险，就像限制电动机固有的风险一样，根本没什么用，只会让电动机变得相对鸡肋。我们希望《AI 法案》能够进一步修订，减轻基础模型构建者（特别是开源项目）的负担，将重心放在解决特定应用中的实际风险，而非其底层技术的「理论」风险。

#### Agentic System for Harder Problems

智能体系统（Agentic System）应对复杂问题

LLMs can struggle with difficult algorithmic or scientific challenges when asked to solve them in a single attempt. An agentic workflow improved one-shot performance on hard problems both theoretical and practical.

当被要求一次性解决时，大语言模型（LLM）在处理复杂的算法（algorithmic）或科学（scientific）挑战时可能会力不从心。一个智能体工作流（agentic workflow）显著提升了它们在理论和实践中解决此类难题的单次尝试（one-shot）性能。

What's new: Alexander Novikov，Ngân Vũ，Marvin Eisenberger，and colleagues at Google built AlphaEvolve，an agentic system that used LLMs to generate code in an evolutionary process. AlphaEvolve solved longstanding math problems and helped to reduce the training time for one of Google's Gemini large language models.

有什么新进展呢？Alexander Novikov、Ngân Vũ、Marvin Eisenberger 和他们在 Google 的同事们开发了一套名为 AlphaEvolve 的 AI 智能体（AI agent）系统。这套系统利用大语言模型（LLM）在一个类似生物进化的过程中生成代码。AlphaEvolve 不仅解决了许多悬而未决的数学难题，还帮助缩短了 Google 旗下某个 Gemini 大语言模型的训练时间。

Key insight: When we're using an LLM to solve a difficult problem，it's often more effective to start with a working version and gradually improve it than to generate a solution in one shot. By making small，targeted modifications and keeping only those that perform best under automated evaluation，this iterative process can solve problems that LLMs often can't solve directly. Google used this idea in its earlier FunSearch，which used an LLM to evolve individual Python functions. This approach has become more powerful as LLMs have improved，and today it can benefit more difficult problems.

一个关键的洞察是：当我们使用大语言模型（LLM）来解决一个复杂问题时，与其一次性生成一个解决方案，不如从一个可用的版本开始，然后逐步对其进行改进，效果会更好。通过进行小而有针对性的修改，并只保留那些在自动化评估中表现最优的方案，这种迭代过程能够攻克大语言模型通常无法直接解决的问题。Google 在其早期的 FunSearch 项目中就运用了这一思路，该项目通过大语言模型来优化单个 Python 函数。随着大语言模型的持续改进，这种方法变得越来越强大，如今它能帮助我们攻克更具挑战性的难题。

How it works: AlphaEvolve implemented an evolutionary loop：Given initial code and evaluation code，Gemini 2.0 Flash and Gemini 2.0 Pro suggested changes，stored the revised program in a database，evaluated it，suggested further changes，and repeated the process.

工作原理：AlphaEvolve 采用了一种进化循环机制：在给定初始代码和评估代码的条件下，Gemini 2.0 Flash 和 Gemini 2.0 Pro 会提出修改建议，随后将修订后的程序存储在数据库中进行评估。接着，它们会根据评估结果进一步提出修改意见，并重复上述过程。

1 The initial code was required to run but it could be minimal，a skeleton with placeholder logic like functions that return constants（such as「def custom_sort(list)：return 2」），which primed AlphaEvolve to find a custom sorting function). Special tags indicated which parts AlphaEvolve could improve (for example,「return 2」only).

初始代码必须能够运行，但它可以非常简洁，就像一个只有占位符逻辑的「骨架」代码，比如那些只返回常量的函数（例如「def custom_sort (list)：return 2」）。这样的设计，可以促使 AlphaEvolve 去寻找一个定制化的排序函数。此外，我们还会用特殊的标签标明 AlphaEvolve 可以优化的部分（比如只标记「return 2」这一行）。

2 The evaluation code could use the usual Python「sorted」function to check for correctness（for instance,「def evaluate()：return custom_sort(lst）== sorted(lst）」).

评估代码时，可以利用 Python 中常见的 「sorted」函数来验证其正确性（例如，通过编写 「def evaluate（)：return custom_sort（lst）== sorted（lst）」）。

3 AlphaEvolve prompted Gemini 2.0 Flash and Pro to improve the code; for example,「Act as an expert software developer. Your task is to iteratively improve the provided codebase. [USER PROVIDED CODE]」. Gemini 2.0 Flash generated ideas quickly， while Gemini 2.0 Pro provided slower but higher-quality suggestions. Each LLM proposed small alterations.

AlphaEvolve 通过向 Gemini 2.0 Flash 和 Gemini 2.0 Pro（大语言模型，LLM）发送提示语，要求它们改进代码；例如，提示语是：「Act as an expert software developer. Your task is to iteratively improve the provided codebase. [USER PROVIDED CODE]」。其中，Gemini 2.0 Flash 能够快速提出改进思路，而 Gemini 2.0 Pro 虽然速度稍慢，但能给出更高质量的建议。每个大语言模型都只进行了小幅的改动。

3 AlphaEvolve ran and scored the altered code using the evaluation code. AlphaEvolve updated a database with the new alterations and their scores.

AlphaEvolve 运行并使用评估代码对修改后的代码进行了评分。随后，AlphaEvolve 用新的修改及其对应的分数更新了一个数据库。

4 The system continued in loop：It sampled high-scoring programs from its database to include in the prompts for the two LLMs，which suggested further alterations. Then it evaluated the altered programs，stored them in the database，and so on. (The authors don't explain how the loop ends.)

该系统持续循环：它从其数据库中抽取高分程序，作为两个大语言模型（LLM）的输入提示，这些模型又会建议进一步的修改。然后，它评估这些修改后的程序，将它们存储到数据库中，依此类推。（作者没有解释该循环如何结束。）

Results: AlphaEvolve achieved breakthroughs in both math and software engineering.

结果：AlphaEvolve 在数学和软件工程领域都取得了突破性进展。

1 AlphaEvolve discovered a new algorithm for multiplying 4×4 matrices of complex values that uses 48 multiplications，fewer than [Strassen's method]，the first such progress in 56 years. (Prior work by Google improved Strassen's method for 4×4 matrices of binary values.)

AlphaEvolve 发现了一种全新的算法，用于计算 4×4 复数值矩阵的乘法，该算法仅需 48 次乘法，比 [Strassen's method] 更少，这是近 56 年来首次取得的此类突破。（此前 Google 在 4×4 二元值矩阵的乘法方面改进了 Strassen's method。）

2 The authors used the system to tackle over 50 other math problems. It matched the performance of the best-known algorithms in about 75 percent of cases and surpassed them in 20 percent，for instance the kissing number problem (packing spheres in 11-dimensional space so they all touch the same sphere).

作者使用该系统解决了 50 多个其他数学问题。在大约 75% 的情况下，它的性能与已知最佳算法（algorithms）持平，甚至在 20% 的情况下超越了它们，例如亲吻数问题（kissing number problem）（在 11 维空间中堆放球体，使所有球体都与一个中心球体相切）。

3 In software engineering，it optimized key components of Google's infrastructure.（i）It improved Google's cluster scheduling algorithms，freeing up 0.7 percent of total computing resources that otherwise would be idle.（ii）It also discovered a GPU kernel configuration that accelerated attention by 32 percent.（iii）It found ways to split up the matrices that delivered an average 23 percent speedup for matrix multiplication relative to previous expert-designed heuristics. This reduced Gemini's training time by 1 percent.

在软件工程领域，它优化了 Google 基础设施的关键组件。（i）它改进了 Google 的集群调度算法，从而释放了 0.7% 的总计算资源，这些资源原本会处于闲置状态。（ii）它还发现了一种 GPU 内核配置，可以将注意力机制（attention）的处理速度提升 32%。（iii）它找到了拆分矩阵的方法，与之前专家设计的启发式方法（heuristics）相比，矩阵乘法平均提速 23%。这使得 Gemini 模型的训练时间减少了 1%。

Why it matters: AlphaEvolve proposes thousands of candidate ideas — some bad，some brilliant — to evolve better programs. The authors show that this approach can improve algorithms that have stood for decades as well as computing infrastructure designed by Google engineers. Thus，AlphaEvolve adds to the growing evidence that LLMs can act as collaborators in cutting-edge research，exploring broad problem spaces and finding novel solutions. Other examples include Co-Scientist and SWE-agent.

为什么它很重要：AlphaEvolve 能够提出数千个候选想法 —— 其中一些可能不太好，但另一些则堪称卓越 —— 从而帮助我们迭代出更优秀的程序。研究人员展示了这种方法不仅可以改进那些沿用了数十年的算法，还能优化 Google 工程师们设计的计算基础设施。因此，AlphaEvolve 为越来越多的证据添砖加瓦，进一步证明了大语言模型（LLM）能够在前沿研究中充当合作者，帮助我们探索广阔的问题空间并找到新颖的解决方案。其他案例还包括 Co-Scientist 和 SWE-agent。

We're thinking: Relatively simple evaluations enabled the authors' agentic evolutionary system to gradually improve. More broadly，evaluations are proving to be important to a wide variety of agentic workflows.

我们发现：相对简单的评估，使得作者们设计的 AI 智能体（AI Agent）进化系统得以逐步改进。更广泛地说，评估正被证明对各种 AI 智能体工作流程都非常重要。
