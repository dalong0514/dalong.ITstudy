## 20250730Trump-Resets-AI-Policy-Qwen3-Agentic-Advance

[Trump Resets AI Policy, Qwen3’s Agentic Advance, U.S. Chips for China, and more...](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/issue-312/)

Dear friends,

There is now a path for China to surpass the U.S. in AI. Even though the U.S. is still ahead, China has tremendous momentum with its vibrant open-weights model ecosystem and aggressive moves in semiconductor design and manufacturing. In the startup world, we know momentum matters: Even if a company is small today, a high rate of growth compounded for a few years quickly becomes an unstoppable force. This is why a small, scrappy team with high growth can threaten even behemoths. While both the U.S. and China are behemoths, China's hypercompetitive business landscape and rapid diffusion of knowledge give it tremendous momentum. The White House's AI Action Plan released last week, which explicitly champions open source (among other things), is a very positive step for the U.S., but by itself it won't be sufficient to sustain the U.S. lead.

中国如今有潜力在人工智能（AI）领域超越美国。尽管美国目前仍处于领先地位，但中国凭借其蓬勃发展的开放权重模型生态系统（open-weights model ecosystem），以及在半导体设计和制造领域的积极布局，正积蓄着巨大的发展势头。在创业圈，我们都深知「势头」的重要性：即使一家公司今天规模尚小，但只要能保持高速增长，假以时日，这种复合式增长很快就会汇聚成一股不可阻挡的力量。这正是为什么一个规模不大但充满活力的团队，若能实现高速增长，甚至可以对那些业界巨头构成威胁。尽管美国和中国都是庞然大物，但中国那种极度竞争的商业环境以及知识的迅速普及，正在赋予其巨大的发展势头。上周白宫发布的 AI 行动计划，其中明确倡导开源（以及其他举措），对美国来说固然是积极的一步，但仅凭此还不足以确保美国持续保持领先地位。

Now, AI isn't a single, monolithic technology, and different countries are ahead in different areas. For example, even before Generative AI, the U.S. had long been ahead in scaled cloud AI implementations, while China has long been ahead in surveillance technology. These translate to different advantages in economic growth as well as both soft and hard power. Even though nontechnical pundits talk about "the race to AGI" as if AGI were a discrete technology to be invented, the reality is that AI technology will progress continuously, and there is no single finish line. If a company or nation declares that it has achieved AGI, I expect that declaration to be less a technology milestone than a marketing milestone. A slight speed advantage in the Olympic 100m dash translates to a dramatic difference between winning a gold medal versus a silver medal. An advantage in AI prowess translates into a proportionate advantage in economic growth and national power; while the impact won't be a binary one of either winning or losing everything, these advantages nonetheless matter.

当前，AI 并非一种单一的、统一的技术，不同国家在不同领域各有所长。例如，即使在生成式 AI（Generative AI）出现之前，美国在大规模云端 AI 部署方面长期领先，而中国则在监控技术领域长期占据优势。这些差异不仅转化为经济增长的优势，也影响着国家在软实力和硬实力上的地位。尽管非技术专家们热衷于谈论「通用人工智能（AGI）竞赛」，仿佛 AGI 是一种有待发明的独立技术，但现实情况是，AI 技术将持续不断地进步，并不存在一个明确的终点线。如果某家公司或某个国家宣称自己已经实现了 AGI，我预计那更像是一个营销上的里程碑，而非真正的技术突破。就像奥运会百米赛跑中微弱的速度优势，就能决定金牌与银牌的巨大差别一样，AI 实力的领先也将为经济增长和国家实力带来成比例的优势。虽然其影响不会是那种非赢即输的二元对立，但这些优势依然至关重要。

Looking at Artificial Analysis and LMArena leaderboards, the top proprietary models were developed in the U.S., but the top open models come from China. Google's Gemini 2.5 Pro, OpenAI's o4, Anthropic's Claude 4 Opus, and Grok 4 are all strong models. But open alternatives from China such as DeepSeek R1-0528, Kimi K2 (designed for agentic reasoning), Qwen3 variations (including Qwen3-Coder, which is strong at coding) and Zhipu's GLM 4.5 (whose post-training software was released as open source) are close behind, and many are ahead of Google's Gemma 3 and Meta's Llama 4 — the U.S.' best open-weights offerings.

审视 Artificial Analysis 和 LMArena 排行榜，我们发现顶级的专有模型大多由美国公司开发，而顶级的开源模型则主要来自中国。Google 的 Gemini 2.5 Pro、OpenAI 的 GPT-4、Anthropic 的 Claude 4 Opus 和 Grok 4 都属于强大的专有模型。然而，来自中国的开源替代品也在迅速崛起，例如 DeepSeek R1-0528、专为 AI 智能体（AI Agent）推理设计的 Kimi K2、以及各种版本的 Qwen3（包括在编程方面表现出色的 Qwen3-Coder）和智谱的 GLM 4.5（其后期训练软件已作为开源发布）。这些中国模型中的许多已经与美国顶级的开源模型，如 Google 的 Gemma 3 和 Meta 的 Llama 4，不相上下，甚至有所超越。

Because many U.S. companies have taken a secretive approach to developing foundation models — a reasonable business strategy — the leading companies spend huge numbers of dollars to recruit key team members from each other who might know the "secret sauce" that enabled a competitor to develop certain capabilities. So knowledge does circulate, but at high cost and slowly. In contrast, in China's open AI ecosystem, many advanced foundation model companies undercut each other on pricing, make bold PR announcements, and poach each others' employees and customers. This Darwinian life-or-death struggle will lead to the demise of many of the existing players, but the intense competition breeds strong companies.

许多美国公司在开发基础模型（foundation models）时，倾向于采取保密策略 —— 这当然是合理的商业考量。因此，领军企业会斥巨资从竞争对手那里挖走核心团队成员，希望他们能带来「秘诀」，从而复制对手的某些成功能力。这样一来，知识确实得以流动，但代价高昂且效率低下。与此形成鲜明对比的是，在中国充满竞争的 AI 生态中，许多先进的基础模型公司为了争夺市场份额，不仅在定价上彼此「内卷」，还大胆发布各种公关声明，甚至互相挖墙脚、抢客户。这种优胜劣汰的「达尔文式」竞争固然会导致不少现有企业的消亡，但激烈的市场搏杀也催生出了一批批真正强大的公司。

In semiconductors, too, China is making progress. Huawei's CloudMatrix 384 aims to compete with Nvidia's GB200 high-performance computing system. While China has struggled to develop GPUs with a similar capability as Nvidia's top-of-the-line B200, Huawei is trying to build a competitive system by combining a larger number (384 instead of 72) of lower-capability chips. China's automotive sector once struggled to compete with U.S. and European internal combustion engine vehicles, but leapfrogged ahead by betting on electric vehicles. It remains to be seen how effective Huawei's alternative architectures prove to be, but the U.S. export restrictions have given Huawei and other Chinese businesses a strong incentive to invest heavily in developing their own technology. Further, if China were to develop its domestic semiconductor manufacturing capabilities while the U.S. remained reliant on TSMC in Taiwan, then the U.S.' AI roadmap would be much more vulnerable to a disruption of the Taiwan supply chain (perhaps due to a blockade or, worse, a hot war).

在半导体（semiconductor）领域，中国也在取得进展。华为的 CloudMatrix 384 旨在与 Nvidia 的 GB200 高性能计算系统竞争。尽管中国在开发与 Nvidia 顶级的 B200 性能相似的 GPU（图形处理器）方面遇到了困难，但华为正试图通过结合更多数量（384 个而不是 72 个）的低性能芯片来构建一个有竞争力的系统。中国的汽车行业曾难以与美国和欧洲的内燃机汽车（internal combustion engine vehicles）竞争，但通过押注电动汽车（electric vehicles）实现了跨越式发展。华为的替代架构（alternative architectures）效果如何仍有待观察，但美国的出口限制（export restrictions）给了华为和其他中国企业巨大的动力，促使它们大力投资开发自己的技术。此外，如果中国发展其国内半导体制造能力（domestic semiconductor manufacturing capabilities），而美国仍然依赖台湾的 TSMC，那么美国的 AI（人工智能）路线图将更容易受到台湾供应链（supply chain）中断的影响（例如可能由于封锁，甚至是一场局部冲突）。

With the rise of electricity, the internet, and other general-purpose technologies, there was room for many nations to benefit, and the benefit to one nation hasn't come at the expense of another. I know of businesses that, many months back, planned for a future in which China dominates open models (indeed, we are there at this moment, although the future depends on our actions). Given the transformative impact of AI, I hope all nations — especially democracies with a strong respect for human rights and the rule of law — will clear roadblocks from AI progress and invest in open science and technology to increase the odds that this technology will support democracy and benefit the greatest possible number of people.

随着电力、互联网以及其他通用技术（general-purpose technologies）的兴起，许多国家都从中受益，一个国家从中受益，并非以牺牲另一个国家为代价。我了解到，有些企业在几个月前就已经规划了中国主导开放模型（open models）的未来 （事实上，我们现在就处于这个时刻，尽管未来取决于我们的行动 ）。考虑到人工智能（AI）的变革性影响，我希望所有国家 —— 特别是那些高度尊重人权和法治的民主国家 —— 能够消除阻碍 AI 进步的障碍，并投资于开放科学和技术，以提高这项技术支持民主、造福尽可能多的人的可能性。

Keep building!

Andrew

#### White House Resets U.S. AI Policy

白宫重新设定美国 AI 政策

President Trump set forth principles of an aggressive national AI policy, and he moved to implement them through an action plan and executive orders.

特朗普总统确立了一系列积极的国家人工智能（AI）政策原则，并随即通过一项行动计划和多项行政命令将其付诸实施。

What's new: In "Winning the Race: America's AI Action Plan," the White House outlines a trio of near-term goals for AI in the United States: (i) stimulate innovation, (ii) build infrastructure, and (iii) establish global leadership. As initial steps in these directions, the president directed the federal government to (a) procure only "ideologically neutral" AI models, (b) accelerate permitting of data-center construction, and (c) promote exports of AI technology.

有什么新进展：白宫在其发布的「赢得比赛：美国的 AI 行动计划」中，为美国人工智能（AI）设定了三项近期目标：（i）激发创新，（ii）构建基础设施，以及（iii）确立全球领导地位。作为落实这些目标的初步举措，总统指示联邦政府采取以下措施：（a）仅采购「意识形态中立」的 AI 模型，（b）加速数据中心建设的审批，以及（c）促进人工智能（AI）技术的出口。

How it works: Rather than advocating for legislation or legal challenges, the plan focuses on actions the executive branch of government can take on its own. President Trump had ordered technology advisor Michael Kratsios, AI advisor David Sacks, and national security advisor Marco Rubio to make a plan to "sustain and enhance America's global AI dominance" within days of starting his current term. Senior policy advisors Dean Ball and Sriram Krishnan, among others, also played key roles.

工作原理：这项计划并没有主张通过立法或法律挑战来解决问题，而是将重点放在政府行政部门能够自行采取的行动上。特朗普总统在本届任期开始的几天内，就已指示技术顾问 Michael Kratsios、AI（人工智能）顾问 David Sacks 以及国家安全顾问 Marco Rubio 共同制定一项计划，旨在「维持并增强美国在全球 AI 领域的领导地位」。此外，高级政策顾问 Dean Ball 和 Sriram Krishnan 等人也在其中发挥了关键作用。

1 Stimulate innovation: The plan would support open-source and open-weights software by boosting U.S. developers' access to processing power and driving adoption of open models by small and medium-size businesses. It calls for the U.S. to build scientific datasets; invest in interpretability, control, robustness, and evaluations; and promote AI in defense applications. In addition, the federal government will support the development of AI skills in its funding of education and workforce training. Moreover, in a speech, Trump said he wants AI companies to be allowed to use copyrighted works freely to train models.

刺激创新：该计划旨在通过提升美国开发者获取算力的能力，并推动中小型企业采纳开放模型，从而支持开源和开放权重（open-weights）软件的发展。它呼吁美国着手构建科学数据集；加大对可解释性、控制、鲁棒性和评估方面的投入；并促进人工智能在国防领域的应用。此外，联邦政府还将通过资助教育和劳动力培训，来支持人工智能人才的培养。值得一提的是，Trump 在一次演讲中表示，他希望人工智能公司能够被允许自由使用受版权保护的作品来训练模型。

2 Build AI Infrastructure: The plan aims to accelerate the building of data centers, semiconductor manufacturing plants, and energy infrastructure. To this end, the federal government will create exemptions to environmental laws, accelerate approvals, and make federal lands available.

构建 AI 基础设施：该计划旨在加快数据中心、半导体制造厂和能源基础设施的建造。为此，联邦政府将为环境法规设立豁免条款，加速审批流程，并开放联邦土地使用。

3 Strengthen global competitiveness: The plan provides for strengthening AI-related export controls, countering the influence of China, and promoting U.S. values in international agreements regarding sensitive technologies such as face recognition. The federal government will coordinate overseas sales of U.S.-made hardware, models, software tools, applications, and standards. To avoid subjecting U.S. companies to a variety of state laws, it will withhold funding from states that pass AI regulations the administration considers burdensome.

加强全球竞争力：这项计划旨在加强 AI 相关出口管制，以应对中国的影响力，并在涉及面部识别等敏感技术的国际协议中推广美国的价值观。联邦政府将协调美国制造的硬件、AI 模型、软件工具、应用程序和相关标准的海外销售。为了避免美国公司受到各州法律的束缚，联邦政府将暂停对那些颁布了被行政部门视为过于严苛的 AI 法规的州的资助。

Behind the news: In contrast to President Trump's emphasis on U.S. dominance in AI, the previous Biden administration focused on limiting perceived risks.

新闻观察：与前总统特朗普强调美国在 AI（Artificial Intelligence）领域的主导地位形成鲜明对比，此前的拜登政府则将重心放在了限制其潜在风险上。

1 In 2023, the Biden administration issued executive orders that required developers to notify government regulators when they built a model that would pose a risk to national security. It advocated legislation that aimed to protect user privacy and prevent AI from discriminating against protected groups.

2023 年，拜登政府发布了一系列行政命令，要求开发者在开发可能对国家安全构成威胁的模型时，必须向政府监管机构报告。此外，政府还积极推动相关立法，旨在保护用户隐私，并防止 AI 对特定弱势群体产生歧视。

2 Biden limited exports of U.S. chips and chip-making technology to numerous countries, notably China but also U.S. allies such as India and Singapore. Trump similarly banned chip sales to China, but reversed course in mid-July and pledged to allow Nvidia and AMD to sell advanced chips to China.

拜登限制了美国芯片和芯片制造技术向多个国家的出口，其中不仅包括中国，也涵盖了印度和新加坡等美国盟友。特朗普曾同样禁止向中国销售芯片，但在七月中旬转变立场，并承诺允许 Nvidia 和 AMD 向中国出售先进芯片。

Why it matters: The Trump administration's action plan sets the stage for U.S. AI developers to do their best work and share their accomplishments with the world. It aims to avoid the European Union's risk-averse regulatory approach and counter China's rising power and influence in AI development. To those ends, it prioritizes a unified national AI policy, streamlines the building of infrastructure, facilitates distributing models and hardware abroad, supports the development of datasets and open-source models, and refrains from defining the arbitrary thresholds of theoretical risk.

Why it matters：特朗普政府的行动计划，旨在为美国 AI（Artificial Intelligence）开发者充分发挥最佳水平、并将他们的成就分享给全世界创造条件。它的目标是避免欧洲联盟（European Union）谨慎保守的监管方式，并应对中国在 AI 发展中日益增长的实力和影响力。为此，该计划优先考虑制定统一的国家 AI 政策、简化基础设施建设、促进模型和硬件向国外推广、支持数据集和开源模型的开发，并避免随意划定理论风险的门槛。

We're thinking: This plan is a positive step toward giving the U.S. the infrastructure, global reach, and freedom from bureaucratic burdens that it needs to continue — and possibly accelerate — the rapid pace of innovation. However, the executive order in support of models that are "objective and free from top-down ideological bias" is wrong-headed. The president complains that some AI models are "woke," and he wants to discourage references to climate change, diversity, and misinformation. But putting those requirements into an executive order, even if it clears some roadblocks to AI development, risks emphasizing some of Trump's own ideological preferences.

我们认为：这项计划是积极的一步，旨在为美国提供所需的基础设施、全球影响力，并使其摆脱官僚障碍，以持续 —— 并可能加速 —— 创新的快速步伐。然而，这项支持「客观且没有自上而下的意识形态偏见」模型的行政命令是存在问题的。总统抱怨一些 AI 模型是「觉醒的（woke）」，并希望不鼓励提及气候变化、多样性和错误信息。但是，将这些要求写入行政命令，即便它能为 AI 发展清除一些障碍，也可能凸显特朗普自己的一些意识形态偏好。

#### Qwen3's Agentic Advance

Qwen3 在 AI 智能体方面的突破

Less than two weeks after Moonshot's Kimi K2 bested other open-weights, non-reasoning models in tests related to agentic behavior, Alibaba raised the bar yet again.

就在月之暗面（Moonshot）的 Kimi K2 在 AI 智能体（AI Agent）行为相关测试中，刚刚击败了其他开源（open-weights）的非推理模型不到两周，阿里巴巴（Alibaba）又一次提高了业界标准。

What's new: Alibaba released the weights for three new large language models based on its earlier Qwen3-235B-A22B. It updated the earlier model (designating the update 2507), divided it into non-reasoning and reasoning variants, and added Qwen3-Coder for coding and multi-turn tool use.

最新进展： 阿里巴巴发布了三款新的大语言模型（LLM），它们都是基于此前发布的 Qwen3-235B-A22B 模型。阿里巴巴不仅更新了原有的 Qwen3-235B-A22B 模型（并将其更新版本标记为 2507），还将其细分为非推理型和推理型两个版本；此外，他们还新增了 Qwen3-Coder 模型，专门用于代码编写和多轮工具调用。

1 Input/output: Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507 and Qwen3-235B-A22B-Thinking-2507: Text in (up to 262,144 tokens), text out (adjustable, up to 32,768 tokens recommended.) Qwen3-Coder: Text in (up to 1 million tokens), text out (adjustable, up to 32,768 tokens recommended).

输入 / 输出能力：对于 Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507 和 Qwen3-235B-A22B-Thinking-2507 模型，它们支持的文本输入 （ Text in ） 最多可达 262,144 token （ Token ），文本输出 （ Text out ） 长度可调，推荐最大为 32,768 token。而 Qwen3-Coder 模型则拥有更长的上下文窗口，文本输入最多可达 100 万 token ，文本输出长度同样可调，建议最大为 32,768 token。

2 Architecture: Mixture-of-experts transformers. Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507 and Qwen3-235B-A22B-Thinking-2507: 235 billion parameters, 22 billion active at any given time. Qwen3-Coder: 480 billion parameters, 35 billion active at any given time.

架构：采用的是专家混合 Transformer（Mixture-of-experts Transformer）架构。其中，Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507 和 Qwen3-235B-A22B-Thinking-2507 模型拥有 2350 亿参数，但在任何特定时刻，只有 220 亿参数处于活跃状态。而 Qwen3-Coder 模型则拥有 4800 亿参数，在任何特定时刻有 350 亿参数处于活跃状态。

3 Performance: Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507: best among non-reasoning models on most benchmarks reported. Qwen3-235B-A22B-Thinking-2507: middling performance compared to proprietary reasoning models. Qwen3-Coder: best among coding models on most benchmarks reported

性能： Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507：在已报告的大多数基准测试中，它在非推理模型（non-reasoning models）中表现最为出色。Qwen3-235B-A22B-Thinking-2507：与那些专有的推理模型（proprietary reasoning models）相比，其性能处于中等水平。Qwen3-Coder：在已报告的大多数基准测试中，它在编码模型中位居榜首。

4 Availability: Free for noncommercial and commercial uses under Apache 2.0 license via HuggingFace and ModelScope, API access via Alibaba Cloud.

可用性：在 Apache 2.0 许可下，模型可通过 HuggingFace 和 ModelScope 免费用于非商业及商业用途，同时也可通过 Alibaba Cloud 平台访问 API。

5 API Price: Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507: $0.70/$2.8 per million input/output tokens. Qwen3-235B-A22B-Thinking-2507: $0.70/$8.4 per 1 million input/output tokens. Qwen3-Coder: $1 to $6 per 1 million input tokens, $5 to $60 per 1 million output tokens depending on the number of input tokens.

API 价格：Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507：每百万输入 token 0.70 美元，每百万输出 token 2.8 美元。Qwen3-235B-A22B-Thinking-2507：每百万输入 token 0.70 美元，每百万输出 token 8.4 美元。Qwen3-Coder：每百万输入 token 价格为 1 至 6 美元，每百万输出 token 价格为 5 至 60 美元，具体取决于输入 token 的数量。

6 Undisclosed: Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507 and Qwen3-235B-A22B-Thinking-2507: updated training data and methods. Qwen3-Coder: training data and methods.

#### People With AI Friends Feel Worse

和 AI 朋友交往，人们反而感觉更糟

People who turn to chatbots for companionship show indications of lower self-reported well-being, researchers found.

研究人员发现，那些转向聊天机器人寻求陪伴的人们，自我报告的幸福感反而有所下降。

What's new: Yutong Zhang, Dora Zhao, Jeffrey T. Hancock, and colleagues at Stanford and Carnegie Mellon examined correlations between users' chatbot usage and psychological health. The more frequently users chatted, shared personal information, and went without human social relationships, the lower they rated their own well-being, the authors found.

最新进展：Yutong Zhang、Dora Zhao、Jeffrey T. Hancock 以及他们在 Stanford 和 Carnegie Mellon 的同事们，深入探究了用户使用聊天机器人（chatbot）的习惯与心理健康之间的关联。研究人员发现，用户与聊天机器人聊天的频率越高、分享的个人信息越多，并且越缺乏人类社交互动，他们自我评价的幸福感就越低。

Key insight: Chatbot users may not report the subject matter of their conversations accurately, but LLMs can identify and summarize topics in chat histories. This makes it possible to correlate the intensity and depth of chats with self-reported measures of well-being, such as loneliness and satisfaction.

一个关键的发现是：聊天机器人用户可能无法准确描述他们聊天的具体话题，但大语言模型（LLM）却能够识别并总结这些聊天记录中的主题。这使得我们可以将聊天互动中的投入程度和交流深度，与用户自我报告的幸福感指标（如孤独感和满意度）进行关联分析。

How it works: The authors surveyed 1,131 users of the chatbot service Character.AI, which provides chatbots for purposes like roleplay, conversation, and education. In addition, they gathered 413,509 messages from 4,363 conversations with 244 participants who agreed to share their chat logs.

这项研究是怎样进行的：研究人员对聊天机器人服务 Character.AI 的 1,131 名用户进行了调查。Character.AI 提供的聊天机器人可用于角色扮演、日常对话和教育等多种目的。此外，研究人员还从 4,363 次对话中收集了 413,509 条消息，这些对话的参与者共有 244 人，他们都同意分享自己的聊天记录。

1 The authors gauged the users' stated motivations for using chatbots versus their likely motivations. They asked the users to select one of the following motivations: productivity, curiosity, entertainment, or companionship. They also asked them to describe freely why they used Character.AI. GPT-4o classified the descriptions of chatbot usage according to the four categories of motivation, giving the authors a more nuanced view.

作者们探究了用户使用聊天机器人（chatbot）的表面动机与潜在动机。他们请用户从以下几种动机中选择一项：提高生产力（productivity）、满足好奇心（curiosity）、寻求娱乐（entertainment）或获得陪伴（companionship）。他们还让用户自由描述了使用 Character.AI 的原因。随后，GPT-4o 将这些描述根据四类动机进行了分类，这使得作者们对用户的动机有了更细致、更深入的认识。

2 They surveyed users to measure the intensity of their chatbot interactions, including how many chatbots they conversed with, how long they conversed, and how comfortable they felt disclosing sensitive personal information.

研究人员对用户进行了一项调查，旨在衡量他们与聊天机器人（chatbot）互动的「强度」，具体包括用户与多少个聊天机器人进行过对话、每次对话持续了多长时间，以及他们在向聊天机器人透露敏感个人信息时感到有多自在。

3 They also surveyed users to measure their human social support, including how many close relationships they had with friends and relatives.

他们还通过调查来衡量用户获得的社会支持，具体包括他们与朋友和亲戚之间有多少亲密关系。

4 Finally, they asked questions to measure the users' well-being based on six factors: satisfaction, loneliness, sense of belonging, positive and negative emotions, and perceived social support.

最后，他们通过提问来评估用户的幸福感，评估依据是六个因素：满意度、孤独感、归属感、积极情绪和消极情绪，以及感知的社会支持。

5 LLaMA-3-70B summarized conversations and fed the summaries to TopicGPT, which identified recurring themes.

LLaMA-3-70B 总结了对话，并将这些摘要提供给 TopicGPT，由其识别出对话中反复出现的主题。

Results: The authors computed correlations among the various signals and the six measures of well-being. They found that most users turned to chatbots for companionship, whether or not they selected companionship as a motivation for their chats. Furthermore, reliance on chatbots for companionship indicated lower well-being.

结果：研究作者计算了各项信号与六项幸福感衡量指标之间的关联性。他们发现，大多数用户都会向聊天机器人寻求陪伴，无论他们是否将「寻求陪伴」明确列为聊天的动机。此外，对聊天机器人（chatbot）陪伴的依赖程度越高，用户报告的幸福感越低。

1 12 percent of users surveyed selected companionship as their primary reason for using Character.AI, but 51 percent described their chatbot as a friend, companion, or romantic partner. 在受访用户中，有 12% 将陪伴选作使用 Character.AI 的主要原因，但实际上有 51% 的用户将他们的聊天机器人描述为朋友、伴侣或恋人。聊天记录进一步揭示，用户将聊天机器人用作伴侣的频率远超预期：93% 的用户至少有过一次表现出伴侣般互动的对话，80% 的聊天会话涉及情感和社会支持，还有 68% 的会话包含浪漫或亲密的角色扮演。

2 Greater use of chatbots for companionship correlated with lower apparent well-being. Yes, but: The authors found a consistent correlation between chatbot companionship and lower well-being, but they didn't establish causation. The data shows that people who sought companionship from chatbots likely struggled with loneliness or a lack of close social connections. It remains unclear whether loneliness caused the users to use chatbots for companionship or vice-versa, or whether using chatbots relieved or exacerbated their loneliness.

没错，但需要指出的是：研究人员发现，寻求聊天机器人（chatbot）陪伴与较低的幸福感之间存在持续的关联，然而这并未能建立因果关系。数据显示，那些倾向于从聊天机器人那里寻找陪伴的人，很可能本身就正与孤独感搏斗，或是缺乏亲密的社交连接。目前仍不清楚，究竟是孤独驱使这些用户寻求聊天机器人陪伴，还是说长期使用聊天机器人反而导致了孤独，又或者，使用聊天机器人究竟是缓解了还是加剧了他们的孤独感。

Behind the news: AI companions have been shown to bring both benefit and harm. Some studies report short-term benefits like reduced loneliness and emotional relief. Users say chatbots are nonjudgmental and easy to talk to. But other work has found emotional overdependence, distorted relationship expectations, and harmful behavior encouraged by unmoderated bots.

新闻背后： 人工智能（AI）伙伴已被证明既能带来益处，也可能造成伤害。一些研究报告指出，它们在短期内能带来好处，例如帮助人们减少孤独感和缓解情绪压力。用户们普遍反映，聊天机器人（chatbots）不会评判对错，让人更容易倾诉。然而，也有其他研究发现，AI 伙伴可能导致用户产生情感上的过度依赖，扭曲对人际关系的期望，甚至在缺乏监管的情况下，这些机器人还可能鼓励有害行为。

Why it matters: Increasingly, people converse with chatbots as an alternative to human conversation. Chatbot builders must be aware of the potential pitfalls of using their products and conduct research sufficient to enable them to build more beneficial bots. Of course, society also has a role to play by fostering social support through access to community, care infrastructure, and mental-health services.

这很重要，因为：如今，越来越多的人选择与聊天机器人（chatbot）对话，将其作为人类交流的一种替代方式。因此，聊天机器人开发者必须充分认识到其产品可能带来的潜在风险，并进行足够深入的研究，从而开发出更有益于用户的聊天机器人。当然，社会也扮演着重要角色，通过提供社区支持、完善的护理基础设施以及健全的心理健康服务，共同促进社会支持体系的建设。

We're thinking: Whether it's beneficial or not, developers are building chatbots that aim to form relationships with people. Such relationships appear to fulfill many of the same needs as human relationships, and they do so in ways that many people, for a wide variety of reasons, find more practical or comfortable. Some developers may be tempted to exploit such needs for profit, but we urge them to design apps that focus on strengthening human-to-human relationships.

我们认为：无论其是否有利，开发者们正在构建旨在与人们建立关系的聊天机器人。这类关系似乎能满足人类关系中的许多需求，而且许多人发现，它们能以各种原因令人觉得更实用或更舒适的方式来满足这些需求。一些开发者可能会受诱惑利用这种需求来牟利，但我们呼吁他们设计出专注于加强人际关系的应用程序（app）。
