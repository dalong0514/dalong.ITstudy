Dear friends,

One of the most effective things the U.S. or any other nation can do to ensure its competitiveness in AI is to welcome high-skilled immigration and international students who have the potential to become high-skilled. For centuries，the U.S. has welcomed immigrants，and this helped make it a worldwide leader in technology. Letting immigrants and native-born Americans collaborate makes everyone better off. Reversing this stance would have a huge negative impact on U.S. technology development.

美国或任何其他国家若想确保其在人工智能（AI）领域的竞争力，最有效的举措之一便是敞开大门，欢迎高技能移民以及有潜力成为高技能人才的国际学生。几个世纪以来，美国一直秉持欢迎移民的政策，这正是其成为全球科技领导者的重要原因。让移民和美国本土人才携手合作，能让所有人都受益。若逆转这一移民政策，将对美国的技术发展造成巨大的负面影响。

I was born in the UK and came to the U.S. on an F-1 student visa as a relatively unskilled and clueless teenager to attend college. Fortunately I gained skills and became less clueless over time. After completing my graduate studies，I started working at Stanford under the OPT（Optional Practical Training）program，and later an H-1B visa，and ended up staying here. Many other immigrants have followed similar paths to contribute to the U.S.

我在英国出生，少年时对很多事情都懵懵懂懂，也没什么技能，便拿着 F-1 学生签证来到美国读大学。幸运的是，随着时间的推移，我不仅掌握了各项技能，也变得更明白事理了。研究生毕业后，我先是凭借「选择性实习训练（OPT）」项目，后来又通过 H-1B 签证开始在斯坦福大学工作，最终便留在了美国。许多其他移民也与我经历相似，通过类似的途径为美国社会贡献着自己的力量。

I am very concerned that making visas harder to obtain for students and high-skilled workers，such as the pause in new visa interviews that started last month and a newly chaotic process of visa cancellations，will hurt our ability to attract great students and workers. In addition，many international students without substantial means count on being able to work under OPT to pay off the high cost of a U.S. college degree. Gutting the OPT program，as has been proposed，would both hurt many international students' ability to study here and deprive U.S. businesses of great talent.（This won't stop students from wealthy families. But the U.S. should try to attract the best talent without regard to wealth.)

我非常担心，如果让学生和高技能人才更难获得签证，将会损害我们吸引优秀学生和人才的能力。比如，从上个月开始暂停新的签证面试，以及最近签证取消流程变得混乱不堪，都属于这类操作。此外，许多经济条件一般的国际学生，都指望着通过可选实习培训（OPT）项目的工作收入，来支付美国大学高昂的学费。正如之前有人提议的那样，大幅削减 OPT 项目，不仅会影响许多国际学生来美国求学，还会让美国企业失去获得优秀人才的机会。（当然，这些政策不会影响来自富裕家庭的学生。但美国应该努力吸引最优秀的人才，而不是只看重财富。）

Failure to attract promising students and high-skilled workers would have a huge negative impact on American competitiveness in AI. Indeed，a recent report by the National Security Commission on Artificial Intelligence exhorts the government to「strengthen AI talent through immigration.」

如果无法吸引到有前途的学生和高技能人才，将会对美国在人工智能（AI）领域的竞争力造成巨大的负面影响。事实上，国家人工智能安全委员会最近发布的一份报告就曾**敦促政府**「通过移民来加强人工智能人才储备」。

If talented people do not come to the U.S.，will they have an equal impact on global AI development just working somewhere else? Unfortunately，the net impact will be negative. The U.S. has a number of tech hubs including Austin，Boston/Cambridge，Los Angeles，New York, Pittsburgh，Seattle，and Silicon Valley，and these hubs concentrate talent and foster innovation.（This is why cities，where people can more easily find each other and collaborate，promote innovation.）Making it harder for AI talent to find each other and collaborate will slow down innovation，and it will take time for new hubs to become as advanced.

Beyond the matter of national competitiveness，there is the even more important ethical matter of making sure people are treated decently. I have spoken with international students who are terrified that their visas may be canceled arbitrarily. One recently agonized about whether to attend an international conference to present a research paper，because they were worried about being unable to return. In the end，with great sadness，they cancelled their trip. I also spoke with a highly skilled technologist who is in the U.S. on an H-1B visa. Their company shut down，leading them — after over a decade in this country，and with few ties to their nation of origin — scrambling to find alternative employment that would enable them to stay.

除了国家竞争力的问题，还有一个更为重要的伦理议题，那就是确保人们得到应有的体面对待。我曾与一些国际学生交流，他们对签证可能被任意取消感到非常恐惧。其中一位最近曾为是否要参加一个国际会议并发表演讲而犹豫不决，因为他们担心可能无法返回美国。最终，他们怀着巨大的遗憾取消了行程。我还与一位持有 H-1B 签证的美国高技能技术人员交谈过。他们的公司倒闭了，这导致他们 —— 在这个国家生活了十多年，并且与原籍国几乎没有联系 —— 不得不仓促寻找能让他们留下的替代工作。

These stories，and many far worse，are heartbreaking. While I do what I can to help individuals I know personally，it is tragic that we are creating such an uncertain environment for immigrants，that many people who have extraordinary skills and talents will no longer want to come here.

这些故事，以及许多更糟糕的遭遇，都令人心碎。虽然我尽力帮助我个人认识的人，但可悲的是，我们正在为移民营造一个如此不确定的环境，以至于许多拥有非凡技能和才华的人将不再愿意来到这里。

To every immigrant or migrant in the U.S. who is concerned about the current national environment：I see you and empathize with your worries. As an immigrant myself，I will be fighting to protect everyone's dignity and right to due process，and to encourage legal immigration，which makes both the U.S. and individuals much better off.

致所有在美国，并关注当前国家环境的移民或迁徙者：我理解并体会你们的担忧。作为一名移民，我将为保护每个人的尊严和获得正当程序的权利而奋斗，并鼓励合法移民，因为合法移民对美国和个人都大有裨益。

#### Apple Sharpens Its GenAI Profile

苹果正在发力生成式 AI（GenAI)

Apple revamped two vision-language models in a bid to catch up with fast-moving competitors.

为了追赶快速发展的竞争对手，苹果公司对其旗下的两款视觉 - 语言模型进行了全面升级。

What's new: Apple updated the Apple Foundation Models（AFM）family，including smaller on-device and larger server-hosted versions，to improve their capabilities，speed，and efficiency. It also released the Foundation Models framework，an API that enables developers to call the on-device model on Apple devices that have Apple Intelligence enabled.

都有哪些新变化呢？苹果公司这次更新了 Apple Foundation Models（AFM）系列，这个系列包含了针对设备端设计的轻量级模型，以及部署在服务器上的大型模型。此次更新旨在全面提升它们的能力、运行速度和效率。此外，苹果还推出了 Foundation Models 框架，这是一个应用程序编程接口（API），它能让开发者在支持 Apple Intelligence 的苹果设备上，轻松调用那些运行在设备端的模型。

1 Input/output: Text，images in（up to 65,000 tokens），text out

输入 / 输出：支持文本和图像作为输入（最多 65,000 个 Token ），输出为文本

2 Architecture: AFM-on-device：3 billion-parameter transformer，300-million parameter vision transformer. AFM-server：custom mixture-of-experts transformer（parameter count undisclosed），1 billion-parameter vision transformer.

架构：在设备端运行的 AFM 模型：包含一个 30 亿参数的 Transformer 模型和一个 3 亿参数的视觉 Transformer 模型。在服务器端运行的 AFM 模型：包含一个自定义的混合专家（Mixture-of-Experts）Transformer 模型（参数量未公布）和一个 10 亿参数的视觉 Transformer 模型。

3 Performance: Strong in non-U.S. English，image understanding

性能：在非美式英语和图像理解方面表现出色。

4 Availability: AFM-on-device for developers to use via Foundations Models framework，AFM-server not available for public use

可用性：开发者可以通过 Foundations Models 框架在设备上使用 AFM 模型；AFM-server 不对公众提供使用。

5 Features: Tool use，15 languages，vision

特点：工具使用能力，15 种语言，视觉能力

6 Undisclosed: Output token limit，AFM-server parameter count，details of training datasets，vision adapter architecture，evaluation protocol

未公开：输出 Token 限制，AFM - 服务器的参数量，训练数据集的详细信息，视觉适配器的架构，评估协议

How it works: Introduced last year，AFM models use a vision encoder to produce an image embedding，which a vision adapter modifies for the LLM. The LLM takes the modified image embedding and text prompt and generates a response. The team trained the systems to predict the next token，align embeddings produced by the vision encoder and LLM，and align responses with human feedback. They trained the models on text and image-text data from publicly available datasets，data scraped from the web，and data licensed from publishers.

工作原理：AFM 模型于去年推出，它首先使用一个视觉编码器来生成图像嵌入，然后由视觉适配器针对大语言模型（LLM）对这些嵌入进行调整。接着，大语言模型接收经过修改的图像嵌入和文本提示，并生成相应的回复。该团队训练这些系统，使其能够预测下一个 Token，同时确保视觉编码器和大语言模型生成的嵌入能够有效对齐，并让模型的回复与人类反馈保持一致。他们利用了多种数据来训练这些模型，包括公开数据集中的文本和图像 - 文本数据、网络抓取数据以及出版商授权数据。

1 Quantization: The team used quantization aware training (simulating quantization during training to improve performance of the quantized model at inference）to compress AFM-on-device to 2 bits per weight（except for the embedding layer，which was compressed to 4 bits per weight). They used Adaptive Scalable Texture Compression，a method initially designed for graphics pipelines，to compress the AFM-server model to an average of 3.56 bits per weight（except for the embedding layer，which is compressed to 4 bits per weight).

量化：该团队采用了量化感知训练（Quantization Aware Training），这种方法能在训练时模拟量化过程，从而提升量化模型在推理阶段的性能。通过这种方式，他们将设备上的 AFM 模型压缩至每个权重只需 2 比特（但嵌入层除外，该层压缩为每个权重 4 比特）。对于 AFM 服务器模型，他们则使用了自适应可伸缩纹理压缩（Adaptive Scalable Texture Compression）技术 —— 这项技术最初是为图形渲染管线设计的 —— 将其平均压缩至每个权重 3.56 比特（同样，嵌入层仍压缩为每个权重 4 比特）。

2 LoRA adapters: They trained LoRA adapters to recover performance loss due to compression，which adapted the model to specific tasks including summarization，proofreading，replying to  email，and answering questions.

LoRA 适配器：研究人员训练了 LoRA 适配器，旨在弥补模型因压缩而下降的性能，这使得模型能够更好地适应特定的任务，例如总结、校对、回复电子邮件以及回答问题。

3 MoE architecture: While AFM-on-device uses a transformer architecture，AFM-server uses a custom mixture-of-experts（MoE）architecture. A typical MoE can be viewed as splitting a portion of its fully connected layers into a number of parallel fully connected layers，of which it uses only a portion at inference. In comparison，the AFM-server's MoE first splits the model into groups of layers，then it splits each group into parallel blocks. Each block is a separate multi-layer transformer outfitted with MoE layers（processed on a small number of hardware devices). While a typical MoE combines results across all devices at every mixture-of-experts layer，Apple's architecture combines them only at the end of each block，which saves communication overhead during processing.

MoE 架构：AFM-on-device 采用 Transformer 架构，而 AFM-server 则使用了定制的混合专家（MoE）架构。通常，我们可以将一个典型的 MoE 理解为将其部分全连接层拆分成多个并行的全连接层，在推理时只调用其中的一部分。与之不同的是，AFM-server 的 MoE 会先将模型划分为不同的层组，然后将每个组再细分成并行的块。每个块都是一个独立的多层 Transformer 模型，并且都配备了 MoE 层（这些层在少量硬件设备上进行处理）。典型的 MoE 会在每一个混合专家层融合所有设备的处理结果，而 Apple 的架构只在每个块处理结束时才进行结果融合，这在处理过程中有效节省了通信开销。

Performance: In human evaluations，the AFM models achieved mixed performance compared to selected models of similar or greater size. The tests included language tasks in U.S. English，non-U.S. English（including Canada and UK），and a basket of European and Asian languages.

Performance：在人类评估中，AFM 模型与选定的大小相似或更大的模型相比，表现有好有坏。测试涵盖了美国英语、非美国英语（包括加拿大和英国），以及一系列欧洲和亚洲语言的语言任务。

1 AFM-on-device: The on-device model performed better than the competitors at language tasks in non-U.S. English and image understanding. For instance，answering questions about images，AFM-on-device bested Qwen2.5-VL-3B more than 50 percent of the time and was judged worse 27 percent of the time.

AFM-on-device：这款端侧（on-device）模型在非美式英语的语言任务和图像理解方面，表现优于其他竞争对手。例如，在回答图片相关问题时，AFM-on-device 在超过 50% 的情况下表现优于 Qwen2.5-VL-3B，并且仅在 27% 的情况下表现逊色。

2 AFM-server: The server model's performance was not decisively better than that of the competitors. For instance，AFM-server outperformed Qwen3-23B 25.8 percent of the time but was judged worse 23.7 percent of the time. It underperformed GPT-4o in all tests reported.

AFM-server：AFM-server 模型的性能并未显著优于竞争对手。例如，AFM-server 在 25.8% 的情况下表现优于 Qwen3-23B，但在 23.7% 的情况下被认为表现更差。在所有已报告的测试中，它的表现均不及 GPT-4o。

Behind the news: Apple dominated social media last week with a controversial paper that purported to show that 5 state-of-the-art reasoning models couldn't solve puzzles beyond a certain level of complexity.

新闻背后：上周，Apple 因一篇颇具争议的论文而刷爆了社交媒体。该论文声称，五种最先进的推理模型无法解决超过某个复杂程度的谜题。

1 The researchers prompted the models with four puzzles that allowed them to control complexity，including swapping the positions of red and blue checkers on a one-dimensional checkers board, Tower of Hanoi, River Crossing，and Blocks World. For all the puzzles and models，they found，the models' performance fell to zero when the puzzles reached a certain degree of complexity（for example，a certain number of checkers to swap).

研究人员向模型提出了四种谜题，这些谜题允许研究人员控制其复杂程度，包括：在一维跳棋盘上交换红色和蓝色跳棋的位置、汉诺塔（Tower of Hanoi）、过河（River Crossing）以及积木世界（Blocks World）。对于所有谜题和模型，他们发现，当谜题的复杂性达到一定程度（例如，需要交换的跳棋达到一定数量）时，模型的性能会直接降至零。

2 A rebuttal paper quickly appeared，penned by Open Philanthropy senior program associate Alex Lawsen with help from Claude 4 Opus. Lawsen contended that Apple's conclusions were unfounded because its tests included unsolvable puzzles，didn't account for token output limits，and posed unrealistic criteria for judging outputs. However，he later posted a blog,「When Your Joke Paper Goes Viral,」in which he explained that he intended his paper as「obvious satire」of authors who use LLMs to write scientific papers，and that he hadn't checked Claude 4 Opus' output. He updated his paper to correct errors in the original version but maintained his fundamental critique.

很快，一篇反驳论文横空出世，这篇论文由 Open Philanthropy 的高级项目助理 Alex Lawsen 在生成式 AI（Generative AI）Claude 4 Opus 的辅助下完成。Lawsen 在文中指出，Apple 的结论站不住脚，原因在于其测试中包含了无解的难题，未考虑到 Token（Token）输出的限制，并且对模型输出的评判标准也过于严苛，脱离实际。然而，他随后发表了一篇题为「当你的玩笑论文走红时」的博客，在其中解释说，他撰写这篇论文的本意，是想「明显讽刺」那些利用大语言模型（LLM）撰写科学论文的作者，而且他当时并未仔细核查 Claude 4 Opus 的输出内容。尽管他后来更新了论文，修正了原始版本中的一些错误，但他依然坚持了对 Apple 的核心批判。

Why it matters: Apple has been viewed as falling behind in AI. A promised upgrade of Siri，Apple's AI assistant，is delayed indefinitely，and the lack of advanced AI features in new iPhones has led to a class-action lawsuit. Meanwhile，Google and its Android smartphone platform are racing ahead. The new models，especially the Foundation Models framework，look like a bid for a reset.

重要性：Apple 在人工智能领域被认为落后了。Apple 的 AI 助手 Siri 承诺的升级被无限期推迟，而新 iPhone 缺乏先进的 AI 功能也导致了一场集体诉讼。与此同时，Google 及其 Android 智能手机平台正在迅速发展，遥遥领先。这些新模型，特别是其中的基础模型（Foundation Models）框架，似乎是为了寻求一次突破性的重新开始。

We're thinking: Apple may be behind in AI，but its control over iOS is a huge advantage. If the operating system ships with a certain model and loads it into the limited memory by default，developers have a far greater incentive to use that model than an alternative. Limited memory on phones and the large size of good models make it impractical for many app developers to bundle models with their software，so if a model is favored by Apple（or Android），it's likely to gain significant adoption for on-device uses.

我们认为：Apple 可能在 AI 方面稍显落后，但它对 iOS 的绝对控制权是一个巨大的优势。如果操作系统预装了某个模型，并默认将其加载到有限的内存中，那么开发者会更有动力使用该模型，而非其他替代模型。由于手机内存有限，加上高质量模型通常体积庞大，许多应用开发者将模型与自家软件捆绑在一起是不现实的。因此，如果一个模型能得到 Apple（或 Android）的青睐，它很可能会在设备端应用中获得广泛采用。

#### Hollywood Joins AI Copyright Fight

好莱坞加入 AI 版权之战

Hollywood studios joined the record companies，publishers，and artists in the fight against companies that have trained AI models on their copyrighted works.

好莱坞电影公司加入了唱片公司、出版商和艺术家们的行列，共同对抗那些利用他们受版权保护的作品训练 AI 模型的公司。

What's new: Disney and Universal sued Midjourney，accusing the image-generation startup of training its models on「countless」unauthorized copies of their copyrighted works and distributing images that depict characters the plaintiffs created.

最新动态：迪士尼和环球影业对 Midjourney 提起诉讼，指控这家图像生成（Image-generation）初创公司在训练其模型时，使用了「无数」未经授权的其受版权保护作品副本，并且分发了描绘原告所创作角色的图像。

How it works: Disney and Universal asked the court to order Midjourney to cease its alleged unauthorized distribution of their intellectual property. Further，they want Midjourney，which took in revenue of $300 million in 2024，to pay unspecified damages based on the claim that copyright law entitles them to $150,000 per infringed image. The studios accuse Midjourney of both direct infringement（that is，directly violating their copyrights by copying，displaying，or distributing their work without permission）and secondary infringement（enabling or encouraging direct infringement by others).

事件始末：迪士尼和环球影业要求法院下令，让 Midjourney 停止其涉嫌未经授权分发自身知识产权的行为。此外，他们还要求 Midjourney 支付未指明的赔偿金。Midjourney 在 2024 年营收高达 3 亿美元，而迪士尼和环球影业则主张，根据版权法规定，每张侵权图像他们有权获得 15 万美元的赔偿。这些工作室指控 Midjourney 存在两种侵权行为：一是直接侵权（即未经许可复制、展示或分发其作品，直接侵犯了他们的版权）；二是次要侵权（通过促成或鼓励他人进行直接侵权）。

#### More Reasoning for Harder Problems

更难题目的更多推理能力

OpenAI launched o3-pro，a more capable version of its most advanced reasoning vision-language model.

OpenAI 推出了 o3-pro，这是其最先进的推理视觉 - 语言模型的一个更强大的版本。

#### LLM Rights Historical Wrongs

大语言模型纠正历史不公

In Northern California，old property deeds may still include racial clauses：language，made illegal decades ago，that was designed to ban people of color from owning or living in certain homes. The state of California now requires counties to find and remove them，but manually combing through millions of documents would take years. Researchers used AI to find them automatically.

在北加州，一些旧的房产地契中可能仍然包含种族条款：这些几十年前就被定为非法的文字，旨在禁止有色人种拥有或居住在某些房屋中。加利福尼亚州现在要求各县查找并移除这些条款，但手动查阅数百万份文件将耗费数年时间。因此，研究人员使用 AI 自动查找这些条款。

What's new: Faiz Surani，Mirac Suzgun，and colleagues at Stanford University and Princeton University fine-tuned a large language model to find racial clauses in deeds for property in the California county of Santa Clara.

新进展：斯坦福大学和普林斯顿大学的 Faiz Surani、Mirac Suzgun 及其同事，微调了一个大语言模型（LLM），专门用于在加利福尼亚州圣克拉拉县的房产契约中查找带有种族歧视性质的条款。

Key insight: Manual and keyword searches may fail to catch racial clauses if they're obscured by subtle wording or errors in optical character recognition（OCR). But a fine-tuned large language model can understand context，identify relevant phrases，and avoid potential false alarms like the surnames Black or White. Lawyers can confirm the model's findings.

关键见解：如果种族条款被微妙的措辞或光学字符识别（OCR）错误所掩盖，手动和关键词搜索可能无法识别出它们。但是，一个经过微调的大语言模型（Large Language Model）可以理解上下文，识别相关短语，并避免潜在的误报（false alarms），诸如姓氏 Black 或 White。律师可以对模型的发现进行确认。

How it works: The authors used an OCR system to extract text from 5.2 million pages of Santa Clara property deeds filed between 1850 and 1980. They drew examples from that corpus to form training and validation datasets and then processed the rest to find deeds that contained racial clauses.

具体方法： 作者们运用光学字符识别（OCR）系统，从 1850 年至 1980 年间归档的 520 万页圣克拉拉县产权契约中提取文本。他们从这些文本资料中抽取样本，构建了训练和验证数据集，随后对剩余资料进行处理，以识别出那些包含种族条款的契约。

1 To curate examples for training and validation，the authors started by sampling 20,000 pages at random. Since deeds have significant variation in format and quality，they added 10,000 deeds from other U.S. counties.

为了准备训练和验证的样本，研究人员首先随机抽取了 20,000 页数据。考虑到地契的格式和质量差异很大，他们又从美国其他县补充了 10,000 份地契。

2 They filtered the combined examples using keywords that may indicate racial clauses，such as「Negro,」「Mongolian,」or「No person of,」yielding 3,801 pages.

他们使用可能包含种族条款的关键词，例如「Negro」、「Mongolian」或「No person of」，对合并后的样本进行了筛选，最终得到了 3,801 页内容。

3 They manually labeled the spans that included such language，which appeared on roughly 80 percent of those pages.

他们手动标注了包含这类表述的文本片段（即「跨度」），这些片段大约出现在这些页面的 80% 上。

4 They fine-tuned Mistral-7B via LoRA on the labeled examples to learn to detect and reproduce discriminatory text.

他们通过 LoRA 技术，利用已标注的案例数据对 Mistral-7B 模型进行了微调，目的是让模型学会识别并生成（复现）含有歧视内容的文本。

Results: The authors fed the remaining roughly 5.2 million unlabeled pages to the fine-tuned model. When the model identified a deed that contained a racial clause，county staff confirmed the finding and redacted the clause.

结果：作者将剩下的大约 520 万页未标注的文件输入到这个经过微调的模型中。当模型识别出包含种族条款的契约（deeds）时，县政府工作人员会核实这一发现，然后对该条款进行涂改或删除处理。

1 The authors found 24,500 Santa Clara lots covered by racial clauses — about one in four homes in the county in 1950.

作者发现，圣克拉拉县有 24,500 块土地受到种族条款的限制 —— 这大约是 1950 年该县房屋总数的四分之一。

2 It also revealed that 10 developers，out of what the authors estimate were hundreds，were responsible for one-third of the racial clauses，demonstrating that only a small number of actors shaped decades of segregation.

研究还揭示，在作者估计的数百名开发商中，仅仅 10 名开发商就制定了三分之一的种族条款，这表明只有少数几个参与者就塑造了长达数十年的种族隔离局面。

3 The fine-tuned model reviewed all pages in 6 days，which would cost an estimated $258 based on current prices for cloud access to GPUs. In contrast，few-shot prompting GPT-3.5 Turbo would have been faster（3.6 days）but less accurate and over 50 times more expensive（$13,634). Working manually，a single county staff member would have needed nearly 10 years and $1.4 million.

经过微调的模型在 6 天内处理了所有页面，根据当前访问 GPU 云服务的价格估算，这将花费 258 美元。相比之下，采用少样本（few-shot）提示的 GPT-3.5 Turbo 速度会更快（3.6 天），但准确性较低，而且成本要高出 50 多倍（13,634 美元）。如果人工处理，一名县工作人员将需要近 10 年时间，花费 140 万美元。

Why it matters: Large language models can interpret historical documents to reveal the nature and scope of actions in the past that otherwise would remain obscure — in this case，housing discrimination. By flagging discriminatory language，this work enables historians to identify areas affected by racial clauses and trace their broader social and economic effects. The team open-sourced the model，streamlining the process for other United States counties.

Why it matters：大语言模型（LLM）能够解读历史文献，从而揭示过去事件的性质和范围，否则这些事件就会模糊不清 —— 比如本案例中的住房歧视。通过标记出歧视性语言，这项工作让历史学家得以识别受种族条款影响的区域，并追溯其更广泛的社会经济影响。该团队还开源了这一模型，从而简化了美国其他县市的工作流程。
