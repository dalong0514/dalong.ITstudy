GPT-5 项目进展遭遇波折

OpenAI launched GPT-5, the highly anticipated successor to its groundbreaking series of large language models, but glitches in the rollout left many early users disappointed and frustrated.

OpenAI 推出了备受期待的 GPT-5，作为其开创性大语言模型（Large Language Model）系列的继任者，然而，发布过程中出现的一些故障让许多早期用户感到失望和沮丧。

What's new: Rather than a family of models, GPT-5 is a family of systems — GPT-5, GPT-5 Mini, GPT-5 Nano, and GPT-5 Pro — that include non-reasoning and variable-reasoning models along with a router that switches between them automatically depending on the input. OpenAI made GPT-5 the only option in the ChatGPT user interface without prior notice, but the router failed right out of the gate, causing the company to reinstate ChatGPT access to earlier models for paid users.

What's new：GPT-5 不再是一个模型系列，而是一个系统家族（family of systems）—— 包括 GPT-5、GPT-5 Mini、GPT-5 Nano 和 GPT-5 Pro。这个系统家族包含了非推理模型（non-reasoning model）和可变推理模型（variable-reasoning model），并搭配了一个路由器（router），能根据输入自动在它们之间切换。OpenAI 在没有事先通知的情况下，将 GPT-5 设置为 ChatGPT 用户界面中的唯一选项，但该路由器一经推出就出现了故障，导致该公司不得不为付费用户恢复了 ChatGPT 访问早期模型的权限。

1 Input/output: Text and images in (up to 272,000 tokens), text out (up to 128,000 tokens including reasoning and response, 122 tokens per second, 72 seconds to first token)

输入 / 输出：支持文本和图像输入（最多可达 272,000 个 token），文本输出（最多可达 128,000 个 token，其中包含推理过程和响应内容；输出速度为每秒 122 个 token，首次 token 输出耗时 72 秒）。

2 Performance: Outperforms previous OpenAI models on most benchmarks reported; tops competing models on some benchmarks of math, coding, and multimodal abilities as well as health knowledge; reduced hallucinations

性能：在大多数已报告的基准测试中，其表现超越了 OpenAI 以往的模型；在数学、编程、多模态能力以及健康知识方面的一些基准测试中，甚至超越了其他竞争模型；同时，模型的「幻觉」现象也大幅减少。

3 Features: Developer options include four levels of reasoning, three levels of verbosity (output length), tool calling via JSON or natural language, selectable non-reasoning and reasoning models, summaries of reasoning tokens

特点：开发者选项涵盖了四大功能：提供四种推理级别；支持三种详细程度（输出长度）；可通过 JSON 或自然语言进行工具调用；允许选择非推理模型和推理模型；以及对推理 Token 进行摘要。

通过 API 接口：

GPT-5 的价格为：每百万输入 Token（Token）$1.25，每百万缓存 Token $0.13，每百万输出 Token $10。

工作原理：OpenAI 鲜有透露关于 GPT-5 架构和训练的细节。他们只提及，为了平衡模型输出的安全性与实用性，模型经过了「安全补全（safe completions）」的微调，而这一技术已在相关论文中有所记载。

#### India Pushes to Build Indigenous AI

印度推动构建本土 AI

India, which has limited funding and large numbers of languages and dialects, is redoubling its efforts to build native large language models.

印度拥有大量语言和方言，并且资金有限，但它正在加倍努力，致力于开发本土的大语言模型（Large Language Model）。

What's new: India is funding startups and marshaling processing resources, MIT Technology Review reported. Companies such as CoRover, Sarvam AI, and Soket AI Labs are working on efficient models that can process many of the 22 officially recognized languages spoken in India while running on relatively small compute budgets.

最新消息显示，据 MIT Technology Review 报道，印度正在大力资助初创企业，并积极整合计算资源。例如，CoRover、Sarvam AI 和 Soket AI Labs 等公司，正在努力开发高效的 AI 模型。这些模型不仅能够处理印度官方认可的 22 种语言中的多种语言，还能在相对较低的计算成本（compute budgets）下高效运行。

Challenges: India is home to more than 120 languages and 19,500 dialects. However, training models to process them faces hurdles both cultural and technical.

挑战：印度拥有超过 120 种语言和 19,500 种方言。然而，训练模型来处理这些语言面临着文化和技术上的挑战。

1 Some Indian languages don't have much written text or consistent spelling, which makes for few large, high-quality datasets for model training.

一些印度语言的书面文本不多，也没有统一的拼写规则，这导致用于模型训练的大型、高质量数据集稀缺。

2 Languages such as Kannada and Tamil can be written without delimiters between words, such as spaces, which makes them difficult to tokenize efficiently.

像卡纳达语和泰米尔语这样的语言，在书写时词语之间没有分隔符（delimiters），例如空格，这使得它们难以高效地进行分词（tokenize）。

3 India lacks the financial muscle available in China, Europe, and the United States. Last year, the U.S. spent 3.5 percent of its gross domestic product on research and development, China spent 2.68 percent, and Europe spent 2.2 percent, while India spent 0.65 percent. The picture is more stark when it comes to funding startups. In 2024, U.S. AI startups amassed $97 billion in venture funding and Europe raised $51 billion, while Indian AI start-ups brought in $780.5 million.

印度缺乏中国、欧洲和美国那样的雄厚财力。去年，美国将其国内生产总值（GDP）的 3.5% 投入研发，中国投入 2.68%，欧洲投入 2.2%，而印度仅投入 0.65%。在为初创企业提供资金方面，这种差距更为显著。2024 年，美国 AI 初创公司获得了 970 亿美元的风险投资，欧洲筹集了 510 亿美元，而印度 AI 初创公司仅获得 7.805 亿美元。

4 India's technology industry has evolved to focus on services, such as those offered by giant software consultant Infosys, Tata, and HCL, rather than products.

印度的科技产业（technology industry）已经发展演变为以服务为核心，而非产品。这体现在许多大型软件咨询公司，例如 Infosys、Tata 和 HCL，都专注于提供服务。

Initiatives: To overcome the challenges, India's government, cloud providers, and startups are attempting to kickstart indigenous model development. Several Indian AI leaders said they're inspired by DeepSeek, the Chinese developer that built a leading large language model while spending far less than its international competitors.

举措：为了克服这些挑战，印度政府、云服务提供商以及众多初创企业正在积极尝试推动本土模型开发。多位印度人工智能（AI）领域的领军人物表示，他们受到了 DeepSeek 的启发 —— 这家中国开发商以远低于国际竞争对手的成本，成功构建了一个领先的大语言模型（LLM）。

1 Last year, India's government approved a $1.2 billion investment in the IndiaAI Mission, an overarching plan to develop AI technology.

去年，印度政府批准了对 IndiaAI 计划的 12 亿美元投资，这是一项旨在开发人工智能（AI）技术的全面计划。

2 Some of that money will bankroll efforts like one by the Indian Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY). In January, just 10 days after DeepSeek released DeepSeek-R1, MeitY called for proposals to build foundation models. #### Training Data for Coding Assistants

用于编程助手的训练数据

A bottleneck in fine-tuning large language models for software engineering is building a dataset that can show them how to edit code, search for subroutines, write test scripts, control a terminal, manage a file system, and so on. Researchers built a pipeline that produces such data automatically.

在针对软件工程对大语言模型（Large Language Model）进行微调（Fine-tuning）时，一个主要的瓶颈在于如何构建一个数据集，来教会它们编辑代码、搜索子程序、编写测试脚本、控制终端、管理文件系统等等。为了解决这一难题，研究人员开发了一条能够自动生成此类数据的流水线。

What's new: John Yang and colleagues at Stanford, Princeton, and Alibaba introduced SWE-smith, a method that generates realistic examples of bug fixes and other code alterations. The code, dataset, and a model that was fine-tuned on the data are freely available for commercial and noncommercial uses.

最新进展：斯坦福（Stanford）、普林斯顿（Princeton）和阿里巴巴（Alibaba）的 John Yang 及其同事，共同推出了一种名为 SWE-smith 的方法，它能够生成逼真的程序错误修复和其它代码修改示例。该方法的代码、相关数据集以及在此数据上微调（fine-tuned）过的模型均已免费开放，可供商业和非商业用途使用。

Key insight: Automated unit tests determine whether code does what it's supposed to do. Code that doesn't pass a unit test has a bug, so one way to generate bug-fix examples is to start with code that passes a unit test and modify it until it doesn't. Another is to start with working code and revert to previous versions that contain bugs or lack desired features. Having introduced issues, we can prompt an LLM to eliminate them, producing valid before-and-after examples that don't require manual validation.

核心思路是：自动化单元测试（automated unit tests）能够验证代码是否按预期运行。未能通过单元测试的代码表明存在错误。因此，一种生成错误修复示例（bug-fix examples）的方法是，从一段能通过单元测试的代码开始，然后对其进行修改，直至其无法通过测试。另一种方法是，从可正常运行的代码开始，然后回溯到包含缺陷或缺少所需功能的早期版本。在引入这些问题之后，我们可以提示一个大语言模型（LLM）来解决它们，从而生成无需手动验证的有效前后对比示例。

How it works: The authors started with 128 GitHub repositories of Python code.

工作原理：研究人员首先准备了 128 个包含 Python 代码的 GitHub 存储库。

1 For each repository, the authors automatically built a Docker execution environment using SWE-agent, an open-source software engineering agent they built in earlier work.

对于每一个存储库，研究人员利用 SWE-agent 自动搭建了一个 Docker 执行环境（Docker execution environment），SWE-agent 是他们在早期的工作中构建的一个开源软件工程智能体（software engineering agent）。

2 They synthesized bugs via four methods: (i) OpenAI o3-mini introduced bugs into functions or classes, (ii) a custom program altered code procedurally; for example, deleting loops or switching the order of lines, (iii) the authors combined these bugs to create more complex problems, and (iv) they reverted pull requests to re-introduce bugs and remove features from earlier versions of the code.

他们通过四种方法来生成 bug（程序错误）：（i）OpenAI o3-mini 将 bug 引入函数或类中，（ii）一个自定义程序通过程序化的方式修改代码；例如，删除循环或切换行的顺序，（iii）作者结合这些 bug 以制造更复杂的问题，（iv）他们回滚了拉取请求，从而重新引入 bug 并移除代码早期版本中的功能。

3 They validated bugs by running unit tests and kept examples in which the buggy code failed one or more tests.To generate examples of multi-step bug fixes, they prompted SWE-agent using Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Claude 3.7 Sonnet, or GPT-4o to fix the bugs over several steps.

他们通过运行单元测试（unit tests）来验证这些程序错误（bugs），并收集了那些错误代码未能通过一个或多个测试的示例。为了生成多步骤错误修复的示例，他们使用 Claude 3.5 Sonnet、Claude 3.7 Sonnet 或 GPT-4o 来驱动 SWE-agent，让它通过多个步骤来修复这些错误。

Results: The authors fine-tuned Qwen 2.5 Coder-32B on 5,000 examples, focusing on the bugs produced by methods (i) and (iv) above, which they found most effective. To represent a diversity of bugs, they kept no more than 3 example fixes for any given bug. Paired with SWE-agent, their model solved software engineering problems in SWE-bench Verified in one attempt 40.2 percent of the time. Paired with the OpenHands agentic framework, the same-size R2E-Gym-32B (fine-tuned on different data) and the much bigger Qwen3-235B-A22B (not fine-tuned) solved 34.4 percent in one attempt.

结果：研究人员将 Qwen 2.5 Coder-32B 模型在 5,000 个示例上进行了微调（fine-tuned），主要侧重于处理前面提到的方法（i）和（iv）所产生的程序错误（bug），因为他们发现这两种方法在生成错误方面最有效。为了确保这些程序错误的多样性，他们对每个特定的程序错误最多只保留了 3 个修复示例。当与 SWE-agent 智能体（AI Agent）结合使用时，他们的模型在 SWE-bench Verified 基准测试中，能够一次性成功解决 40.2% 的软件工程问题。而当与 OpenHands 智能体驱动的框架搭配使用时，同样大小的 R2E-Gym-32B（在不同数据上微调过）和规模更大的 Qwen3-235B-A22B（未经过微调）则能一次性解决 34.4% 的问题。

Why it matters: Previous datasets for fine-tuning LLMs on coding tasks are small, often comprising thousands of training instances from less than a dozen repositories. The authors' method can produce such data at scale, potentially enabling major developers to improve their AI-assisted coding models and everyone else to build better systems.

为何重要：以前用于在编码任务上微调大语言模型（LLM）的数据集规模很小，通常只包含来自不到十几个代码库的数千个训练实例。作者的方法能够大规模生成这类数据，这有望帮助主要开发者改进他们的 AI 辅助编码模型，同时也能赋能其他人构建更优异的系统。
