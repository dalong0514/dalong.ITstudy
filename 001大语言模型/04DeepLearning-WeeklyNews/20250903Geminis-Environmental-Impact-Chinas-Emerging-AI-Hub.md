掌握这些技能的人，其工作效率远超那些沿用 2022 年生成式 AI（Generative AI）出现之前编码方式的开发者。我每周都会接触到许多大型企业，他们都非常渴望招聘数百甚至更多的此类人才；同时，也有许多初创公司虽然创意绝佳，却苦于没有足够的工程师来实现它们。随着 AI 被越来越多的企业采纳，我预计这种人才短缺只会日益严重。然而，与此同时，近期的计算机科学毕业生却面临着更高的失业率（例如，参考这项基于 2023 年数据的研究），尽管他们从事无需学位的工作（即未充分就业）的比例仍低于大多数其他专业。这解释了为什么我们一方面会听到计算机科学毕业生找不到工作的传闻，另一方面又会看到市场对高需求 AI 工程师的薪资报价不断上涨。

如果你不了解计算机是如何工作的，那么光靠「凭感觉编程」（vibe code）是无法让你取得巨大成功的。扎实的基础知识依然至关重要。更棒的是，对于那些同时掌握了 AI （人工智能）知识的人来说，就业机会可谓是数不胜数！

#### Chatbot Interviewers Fill More Jobs

聊天机器人面试官助力招聘更多岗位

Large language models may have advantages over human recruiters when conducting job interviews, a study shows.

一项研究表明，大语言模型（Large language models）在进行求职面试时，可能比人类招聘人员更具优势。

What's new: Researchers at the University of Chicago and Erasmus University Rotterdam found that, relative to interviews by recruiters, AI-led interviews increased job offers, acceptances, and retention of new employees.

最新进展：芝加哥大学（University of Chicago）和鹿特丹伊拉斯谟大学（Erasmus University Rotterdam）的研究人员发现，与招聘人员进行的面试相比，由 AI 主导的面试提高了工作机会数量、员工接受度以及新员工的留存率。

How it works: The authors collected interviews with roughly 67,000 qualified applicants for nearly 50 job openings in a range of industries. The jobs were mostly entry-level, customer-service positions located in the Philippines that offered monthly compensation between $280 and $435. Interviewees were either assigned to the recruiter, assigned to the chatbot, or given a choice between the two. The chatbot was Anna AI, a large language model with voice input/output from the recruiter PSG Global Solutions.

工作原理：这项研究中，作者们对约 67,000 名合格申请者进行了面试。这些申请者应聘了来自多个行业的近 50 个职位空缺。这些职位大部分是位于菲律宾的入门级客户服务岗位，月薪在 280 美元至 435 美元之间。面试者被分为三组：一组由招聘人员面试，一组由聊天机器人面试，另一组则可以在两者之间自由选择。这个聊天机器人就是 Anna AI，一个由招聘公司 PSG Global Solutions 提供语音输入 / 输出功能的大语言模型（Large Language Model）。

1 All interviews followed the same format: Applicants were asked about career goals, education, and experience and were allowed to ask questions afterward. Both the recruiter and Anna AI were permitted to ask follow-up questions.

所有面试都遵循相同的格式：面试官会询问申请人的职业目标、教育背景和工作经验，申请人在之后也可以提问。招聘人员和 Anna AI 都被允许提出追问。

2 Following the interviews, around 2,700 applicants completed a survey designed to measure their satisfaction with the interview process and general attitudes toward AI.

面试结束后，约 2,700 名申请者完成了一项调查，这项调查旨在评估他们对面试流程的满意度，以及他们对 AI（Artificial Intelligence）的总体看法。

3 Human recruiters made all hiring decisions after assessing interviews via audio recordings, interview transcripts, and standardized test scores. They were instructed to apply the same assessment criteria to each hire regardless of whether the applicant was interviewed by a recruiter or Anna AI.

人力招聘人员根据音频记录、面试笔录和标准化考试分数来评估面试表现，并做出了所有招聘决定。他们被要求对每一次招聘都采用相同的评估标准，无论申请人是由招聘人员还是由 Anna AI 进行面试。

Results: The authors found that AI can yield more hires, seem more unbiased, and put applicants more at ease than human interviewers.

结果：研究人员发现，AI 不仅能促成更多录用，看起来也更公正，而且相比人类面试官，还能让申请者感到更放松。

1 Job applicants that were interviewed by Anna AI were 12 percent more likely to be offered a job than those who were interviewed by a recruiter. Among applicants who received an offer, those who had been interviewed by Anna AI were 18 percent more likely to start the job.

与招聘人员面试的求职者相比，被 Anna AI 面试的求职者获得工作机会的几率高出 12%。在所有收到录用通知的申请者中，那些曾被 Anna AI 面试过的人，最终入职的可能性高出 18%。

2 In a free-form survey, applicants interviewed by Anna AI were half as likely to report that the interviewer discriminated against them based on their gender.

在一项自由形式的调查中，由 Anna AI 面试的申请者报告面试官（interviewer）基于性别歧视（discriminated against）他们的可能性减少了一半。

3 Around 5 percent of AI interviews ended early, and 7 percent had technical difficulties.

大约 5% 的 AI 面试提前结束，7% 遇到了技术难题。

4 On the other hand, Anna AI covered a median of 9 topics while recruiters covered 5, and applicants interviewed by Anna AI were 71 percent more likely to give a positive assessment of the interview experience.

另一方面，Anna AI 平均会涉及 9 个话题，而招聘人员通常只涉及 5 个。此外，由 Anna AI 面试的求职者对面试体验给出积极评价的可能性高出 71%。

Behind the news: The rise of AI software that performs job interviews has raised concerns that such systems may be biased against certain demographic characteristics. Some U.S. states have moved to limit some uses of AI in hiring. Meanwhile, job seekers are turning the tables on employers by using a variety of AI models to make a better impression during interviews.

新闻背后：能够进行工作面试的 AI 软件（AI software）日益普及，这引发了人们的担忧，认为此类系统可能对某些特定人群存在偏见。为此，一些美国州已着手限制 AI 在招聘中的某些应用。与此同时，求职者们也开始「反客为主」，利用各种 AI 模型在面试中给雇主留下更好的印象。

Why it matters: Many discussions of AI-powered job interviews focus on the potential for bias, but few point out the technology's benefits for applicants and employers alike. This study found that chatbot interviews can contribute to a win-win situation: More applicants hired and fewer quick departures. The study covered the relatively narrow realm of call-center jobs, and its conclusions may not apply more broadly. But it suggests that chatbot interviews may have advantages beyond convenience and cost.

这很重要，因为： 许多关于 AI 驱动的求职面试的讨论都集中在其潜在的偏见上，但很少有人提及这项技术对求职者和雇主双方都有哪些好处。这项研究发现，聊天机器人面试有助于实现双赢： 既能让更多求职者成功入职，又能减少员工快速离职的情况。虽然这项研究仅限于呼叫中心这类相对狭窄的领域，其结论可能不具有普适性，但它提示我们，聊天机器人面试的优势可能远不止方便和节省成本。

We're thinking: Job applicants in this study felt the chatbot was less biased when it came to gender. Today more tools are available for reducing AI bias than human bias! Technologists' work is clearly paying off in this area.

我们发现：本研究中的求职者认为，聊天机器人在涉及性别问题时，表现出的偏见更少。如今，用于减少 AI 偏见的工具，甚至比减少人类偏见的工具还要多！显然，技术人员在这个领域的工作正在卓有成效地开展。

#### China's Emerging AI Hub

中国崛起中的人工智能中心

Hangzhou, a longtime manufacturing hub in eastern China, is blossoming into a center of AI innovation.

杭州，这个中国东部地区久负盛名的制造业基地，正在崛起成为一个人工智能（AI）创新中心。

What's new: The rise of DeepSeek and other AI companies that are among the "6 little dragons of Hangzhou" has raised the city's profile as a technology hotbed. Hangzhou's ability to produce AI leaders — not only the dragons but also Alibaba, Hikvision, NetEase, and Rokid — has generated headlines.

新进展：DeepSeek 及其他同属「杭州六小龙」的人工智能（AI）公司的崛起，提升了杭州作为科技热点的知名度。杭州在培养 AI 领域领军企业方面的实力 —— 不仅包括这些「小龙」，还有阿里巴巴、海康威视、网易和 Rokid 等 —— 已经成为媒体关注的焦点。

Dragons: The 6 little dragons include five AI companies: BrainCo, Deep Robotics, DeepSeek, ManyCore, and Unitree Robotics. (The sixth is the hit game developer Game Science.)

「小龙」企业：这六家「小龙」企业包括五家人工智能（AI）公司：BrainCo、Deep Robotics、DeepSeek、ManyCore 和 Unitree Robotics。(第六家则是热门游戏开发商 Game Science。)

1 BrainCo started in a Boston garage in 2015, when Bicheng Han was pursuing a PhD at Harvard. Hangzhou offered him funds to rent property, and he moved there in 2018. It makes brain-computer interfaces designed for meditation and sleep using AI to interpret brain signals.

BrainCo 于 2015 年在波士顿的一个车库里创立，彼时创始人韩璧丞正在哈佛大学攻读博士学位。2018 年，杭州向他提供了租金资助，他因此将公司迁至杭州。BrainCo 致力于研发用于冥想和睡眠的脑机接口（brain-computer interfaces），这些设备利用人工智能（AI）来解读大脑信号。

2 Deep Robotics was founded in 2017 by Zhu Qiuguo and Li Chao. It makes quadruped robots that navigate autonomously for industrial uses and rescue missions. Singapore Power Group uses its X30 robot to inspect power tunnels.

Deep Robotics 公司由 Zhu Qiuguo 和 Li Chao 于 2017 年创立。该公司制造能够自主导航（navigate autonomously）的四足机器人，应用于工业和救援任务。Singapore Power Group 目前使用其 X30 机器人来检查电力隧道。

3 Founded in 2023 by Liang Wenfeng, DeepSeek is an independent subsidiary of the AI-powered investment firm High-Flyer Capital Management. The company has focused on building open-weights models, including DeepSeek-R1, that famously rival top closed models but cost much less to develop.

DeepSeek 公司由梁文峰于 2023 年创立，是人工智能（AI）投资公司 High-Flyer Capital Management 旗下的一个独立子公司。该公司一直致力于开发开放权重模型（open-weights models），其中 DeepSeek-R1 系列模型就备受瞩目。这些模型以其出色的性能而闻名，不仅能与顶级的闭源模型相媲美，而且开发成本也低得多。

4 ManyCore was founded in 2011 by Huang Xiaohuang, Chen Hang, and Zhu Hao. In 2023, its 3D design platform, which uses AI to generate and manipulate virtual scenes, was the world's largest by monthly active users, and China's largest by revenue. It applied for a public offering on the Hong Kong stock exchange in early 2025.

ManyCore 公司由 Huang Xiaohuang、Chen Hang 和 Zhu Hao 于 2011 年创立。2023 年，该公司利用人工智能（AI）生成和操纵虚拟场景的 3D 设计平台，凭借其月活跃用户数成为全球最大的平台，同时也是中国收入最高的平台。ManyCore 已于 2025 年初申请在香港证券交易所公开募股。

5 Unitree Robotics, maker of acrobatic humanoid robots, was founded in 2016 by Wang Xingxing. Today it accounts for 60 percent of the quadruped robot market and also produces humanoid robots. It's valued at $1.4 billion.

Unitree Robotics 是一家以制造高难度「杂技」动作的人形机器人（humanoid robots）而闻名的公司，由王兴兴于 2016 年创立。如今，它不仅生产人形机器人，还在四足机器人（quadruped robot）市场占据了高达 60% 的份额。目前，该公司的估值已达到 14 亿美元。

Lessons: Shenzhen and Beijing have been called "China's Silicon Valley," but lately Hangzhou has started to eclipse them, largely by providing startups with tax breaks and subsidies, maintaining talent pipelines, encouraging collaboration between private and public sectors, and spending on computing resources and other infrastructure. Hangzhou's recent Future Industries Development Plan (2025–2026) focuses on AI and robotics as well as synthetic biology.

经验启示：深圳和北京曾被誉为「中国的硅谷」，但最近杭州已经开始崭露头角，甚至盖过了它们的风头。这主要得益于杭州为初创企业提供了税收减免和补贴、保持了人才的持续供给、鼓励了私营部门与公共部门之间的合作，并大力投入计算资源及其他基础设施建设。杭州近期发布的《未来产业发展规划（2025-2026）》明确将重心放在人工智能（AI）、机器人技术以及合成生物学等前沿领域。

1 Hangzhou allocates 15 percent of the city's annual fiscal revenue to tech investments. For instance, when Game Science ran out of office space, the city secured space and kept two buildings vacant for three years in case Game Science needed them.

杭州将其年度财政收入的 15% 用于技术投资。例如，当 Game Science 办公空间不足时，市政府就为他们腾出了空间，甚至预留了两栋空置的建筑长达三年，以备 Game Science 未来不时之需。

2 The city benefits from the presence of Zhejiang University, which feeds talent to local companies. Zhejiang alumni founded 4 of the 6 dragons. Graduates looking for work in Hangzhou can spend a week in government-managed accommodations, free of charge. For those who qualify as high-level talent, Hangzhou supplements housing costs and daily expenses with hundreds of thousands of RMB.

杭州这座城市得益于浙江大学的强大支持，源源不断地为本地企业输送优秀人才。值得一提的是，当地「六条龙」企业中，有四家都是由浙江大学的校友创办的。对于到杭州求职的毕业生，政府会提供免费的周转住宿，可居住一周。而对于符合条件的高层次人才，杭州更是大手笔，会提供数十万元人民币的住房和日常开销补贴。

3 Alibaba Cloud, China's largest cloud platform, provides computing power to startups, ThinkChina reported. In addition, many companies have stockpiles of Nvidia GPUs, supplemented by homegrown processors from Huawei and Semiconductor Manufacturing International Corporation.

据 ThinkChina 报道，作为中国最大的云平台，阿里云为初创公司提供强大的计算能力。此外，许多公司不仅储备了大量的 Nvidia 图形处理器（GPUs），还配有来自华为和中芯国际（Semiconductor Manufacturing International Corporation）的国产处理器。

Why it matters: The world needs many AI centers, and Hangzhou is bringing its own distinctive character to AI development.

重要意义在于：世界需要众多 AI（人工智能）中心，而杭州正在为 AI 发展注入其独有的特色。

We're thinking: In the U.S., tech companies are concentrated in a few cities, notably in Northern California. But as countries across the globe venture into AI, they would be wise to try and establish multiple hubs.

我们的思考是：在美国，科技公司主要集中在少数几个城市，特别是北加州。但随着全球各国纷纷进军 AI 领域，明智之举是尝试建立多个 AI 枢纽。

#### Gemini's Environmental Impact Measured

Gemini 的环境影响衡量结果

Google determined that its large language models have a smaller environmental footprint than previous estimates had led it to expect.

Google 发现，其大语言模型（Large Language Model）造成的环境足迹比之前预期的要小。

What's new: For one year, Google researchers studied the energy consumption, greenhouse gas emissions, and water consumption of the models that drove its Gemini AI assistant in applications like Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Flights, and Maps. (They didn't identify the specific models involved.) They found that the impact of processing a single prompt was roughly comparable to loading a web page or streaming a brief video to a television screen.

最新进展：Google 研究人员对驱动其 Gemini AI（人工智能）助手在 Gmail、Calendar、Drive、Flights 和 Maps 等应用中使用的模型进行了为期一年的研究，内容涵盖这些模型的能源消耗、温室气体排放和水消耗。（研究人员并未公开具体涉及的模型。）他们发现，处理单个提示（prompt）所产生的影响，大致相当于加载一个网页，或者向电视屏幕播放一小段视频。

How it works: The authors confined their study to inference in text-processing tasks, calculating the impact of processing a single "median" prompt (one that consumes the median amount of energy across all prompts and models). They considered only activities under Google's operational control, including data-center construction and hardware manufacturing, but not including internet routing or end-user devices.

工作原理：作者们将研究范围限定在文本处理任务中的推理环节，并计算了处理单个「中位数」提示（即在所有提示和模型中，其能耗处于中间水平的提示）所产生的影响。他们只考虑了 Google 运营控制下的活动，这包括数据中心的建设和硬件制造，但不包括互联网路由或最终用户设备。

1 Energy: The authors measured energy used to classify prompts, route them to specific models, and rank potential responses. To accomplish this, they traced the hardware used and measured energy consumption of all hardware components within a server rack, including idle machines, active processors, and cooling systems. TPUs, Google's custom AI processors, accounted for 58 percent of the total energy consumption.

能源：研究人员测量了分类提示、将提示路由到特定模型以及对潜在响应进行排序所消耗的能量。为此，他们追踪了所使用的硬件，并测量了服务器机架内所有硬件组件的能耗，这其中包括了空闲设备、运行中的处理器和冷却系统。其中，Google 定制的 AI 处理器（TPU）占总能耗的 58%。

2 Emissions: The authors calculated greenhouse gas emissions by multiplying the energy consumed per median-length prompt by the previous year's average emissions per unit of electricity plus operational emissions from sources like heating and air conditioning as well as embodied emissions like hardware manufacturing and transportation, and building the data center itself. They estimated operational and embodied emissions using results from this study.

排放量：作者计算温室气体排放时，采用的方法是：先将每个中等长度提示（prompt）所消耗的能量，乘以去年每单位电力的平均排放量；再将这一结果，加上数据中心运营产生的排放（例如供暖和空调），以及其隐含的排放（例如硬件制造、运输以及数据中心自身的建设）。其中，运营排放（operational emissions）和隐含排放（embodied emissions）的估算数据来源于这项研究。

3 Water: Water is used to cool data-center hardware, and around 80 percent of it evaporates. The authors measured water input minus water returned in 2023 and 2024. This enabled them to calculate water usage per energy (1.15 liter per kilowatt hour), which they multiplied by the amount of energy used per prompt to calculate the water usage per prompt.

水：水被用来冷却数据中心的硬件，其中约 80% 会蒸发掉。作者们测量了 2023 年和 2024 年的进水量与回水量之差。这使得他们能够计算出单位能耗（能源消耗）的用水量（每千瓦时 1.15 升）。他们将这个数值乘以每次提示（prompt）所消耗的能量，从而计算出每次提示的用水量。

Results: The energy and water consumed and greenhouse gases emitted by Gemini AI assistant's models fell well below Google's estimates in previous years. Moreover, between May 2024 and May 2025, given a median prompt, the models' energy consumption fell by a factor of 33 and their greenhouse gas emissions fell by a factor of 44, reductions attributable to clean-energy procurement and more energy-efficient hardware and software.

结果：Gemini AI 助手（AI assistant）模型的能源和水消耗量以及温室气体排放量，远低于 Google 往年的估算。此外，在 2024 年 5 月至 2025 年 5 月期间，在中位数提示（median prompt）的条件下，模型的能源消耗量减少到原来的 1/33，温室气体排放量则减少到原来的 1/44。这些显著的降低得益于清洁能源的采购，以及更节能的硬件和软件。

1 A median text prompt consumed approximately 0.24 watt-hours, around the amount of energy that a television screen consumes over 9 seconds.

一个文本提示（中位数）大约消耗 0.24 瓦时，这大约相当于一台电视屏幕 9 秒的耗电量。

2 The median prompt consumed 0.26 milliliters of water, about five drops.

一个提示（中位数）消耗了 0.26 毫升的水，大约是五滴。

3 Each median prompt generated about 0.03 grams of greenhouse gases, roughly the amount emitted when loading a single webpage.

平均每个提示生成了大约 0.03 克温室气体，这大致相当于加载一个网页时所产生的排放量。

Behind the news: Recently, Mistral assessed its Mistral Large 2 model in a similar way (although its study included training). It found that, at inference, 400-token prompt generated 1.14 grams of greenhouse gases and consumed 45 milliliters of water.

拓展阅读：最近，Mistral 也以类似的方式评估了其 Mistral Large 2 模型（尽管 Mistral 的研究涵盖了模型训练阶段）。他们发现，在推理时，一个 400 个 Token（Token）的提示会产生 1.14 克温室气体，并消耗 45 毫升水。

Yes, but: Earlier research arrived at measurements as much as two orders of magnitude higher than Google's, largely because they included factors that Google did not, The Verge reported. For instance, a 2023 study found that GPT-3 used about 10 milliliters to 50 milliliters of water per (average) prompt — greater than Google's Gemini findings by 40 to 200 times. That study included water used in generating electricity, such as steam used to turn turbines or water used to cool nuclear generators, which Google omitted. Further, the 2023 study based its estimate of greenhouse gas emissions on actual emissions of local grids, while Google based its measurement on the company's commitments to buy energy from low-carbon sources. Google did not respond to questions from The Verge.

不过，早期的研究得出的测量结果，却比 Google 的高出了足足两个数量级。据 The Verge 报道，这主要是因为这些研究在计算时，考虑了 Google 并未计入的因素。举例来说，一项 2023 年的研究发现，GPT-3 模型平均每次处理一个提示（prompt）大约会消耗 10 到 50 毫升的水 —— 这个数字是 Google Gemini 模型报告结果的 40 到 200 倍。这项研究将发电过程中消耗的水也计算在内，比如用于驱动涡轮机的蒸汽或冷却核发电机的水，而 Google 则没有计入这些。此外，这项 2023 年的研究是根据当地电网的实际碳排放来估算温室气体排放量，而 Google 则是依据其购买低碳能源的承诺来得出测量结果的。Google 没有回应 The Verge 的相关提问。

Why it matters: Assessing the environmental cost of AI has proven to be difficult, and different approaches paint very different pictures. Google's approach has the benefit of focusing on variables under its control and addressing energy, greenhouse gases, and water. However, it leaves out important contributors to these measures — including training — as well as consumption of materials, as highlighted in Mistral's assessment.

为什么这很重要：评估人工智能（AI）的环境成本一直是个难题，不同的评估方法会得出截然不同的结果。Google 的方法有一个优点，那就是它专注于自身可控的变量，并涵盖了能源、温室气体和水等方面的考量。然而，它也遗漏了对这些衡量指标有重要影响的因素，比如模型训练过程，以及材料的消耗，正如 Mistral 的评估报告所指出的那样。

We're thinking: The AI industry needs a standard method that would enable AI companies to report their models' environmental impacts and the public to compare them. Kudos to Google, Mistral, and the independent researchers for proposing practical approaches and continuing to refine them.

我们认为，人工智能（AI）行业需要一种标准方法，让各家 AI 公司能够报告其模型的环境影响，也方便公众进行比较。Google、Mistral 以及独立的科研人员为此提出了实用方案并持续改进，这种努力值得我们称赞。

#### Cybersecurity for Agents

智能体的网络安全

Autonomous agents built on large language models introduce distinct security concerns. Researchers designed a system to protect agents from common vulnerabilities.

基于大语言模型（Large Language Model）构建的自主智能体（Autonomous Agent）带来了独特的安全挑战。研究人员为此设计了一个系统，旨在保护这些智能体免受常见漏洞的侵害。

What's new: Sahana Chennabasappa and colleagues at Meta released LlamaFirewall, an open-source system designed to mitigate three lines of attack: (i) jailbreaking (prompts that bypass an LLM's built-in safeguards), (ii) goal hijacking (inputs that aim to change an LLM's prompted goal), and (iii) exploiting vulnerabilities in generated code. The code and models are freely available for projects that have up to 700 million monthly active users.

最新消息：Sahana Chennabasappa 和她在 Meta 的同事们发布了 LlamaFirewall，这是一个开源系统，旨在抵御三类攻击：（i）越狱（jailbreaking）（即绕过大语言模型（Large Language Model，LLM）内置安全防护的提示），（ii）目标劫持（goal hijacking）(即旨在改变大语言模型预设目标的输入），以及（iii）利用生成代码中的漏洞（exploiting vulnerabilities in generated code). 该系统包括的代码和模型可以免费用于每月活跃用户高达 7 亿的项目。

Key insight: Security for LLMs typically focuses on filtering inputs and fine-tuning outputs. But agentic LLMs retain vulnerabilities that aren't addressed by those techniques and present new ones as well. Receiving instructions exposes them to jailbreaking, tool use makes them vulnerable to goal hijacking (for instance, when an agent conducts a web search and encounters malicious data), and output code may open security holes outside the agent itself. To defend against these weaknesses, a security system can filter malicious prompts, monitor chains of thought for deviations from prompted goals, and check generated code for flaws.

核心观点是：大语言模型（LLM）的安全性通常集中于过滤输入和微调输出。然而，AI 智能体大语言模型（agentic LLM）不仅存在现有技术无法解决的漏洞，还带来了新的安全风险。它们在接收指令时可能遭遇越狱（jailbreaking）攻击；工具的使用使得它们容易受到目标劫持（goal hijacking），例如当一个智能体执行网页搜索并接触到恶意数据时；此外，其生成的代码也可能在智能体外部引入安全漏洞。为了应对这些弱点，一个安全系统可以过滤恶意提示，监控思维链（chains of thought）是否偏离预设目标，并检查生成代码中的潜在缺陷。

How it works: LlamaFirewall integrates three modules:

工作原理：LlamaFirewall 集成了三个模块：

1 PromptGuard 2: To block malicious inputs, DeBERTa, an 86 million parameter transformer fine-tuned to classify prompts as benign or malicious, classifies incoming text from users or external tools.

PromptGuard 2：为了有效阻止恶意输入，LlamaFirewall 使用了 DeBERTa 模型。这个拥有 8600 万参数的 Transformer 模型经过了专门微调，能够将来自用户或外部工具的输入文本（即提示）区分为无害或有害。

2 AlignmentCheck: To detect goal hijacking, Llama 4 Maverick compares chains of thought, tool calls, and output with the user's objective as stated in the initial prompt. If the generated text or tool calls drift away from the user's intended objective, LlamaFirewall stops the generation.

AlignmentCheck：为了检测「目标劫持（goal hijacking）」现象，Llama 4 Maverick 会将模型的「思维链（chains of thought）」、进行的「工具调用（tool calls）」和最终输出与用户在初始提示中设定的目标进行比对。一旦检测到生成的文本或工具调用偏离了用户原本的目的，LlamaFirewall 就会立即停止生成过程。

3 CodeShield: To check generated code for flaws, this module uses rules to detect insecure patterns in generated code, such as vulnerability to SQL injections (like "SELECT * FROM users WHERE email LIKE '" + domain + "'", which allows SQL injections through the unsanitized input parameter  "domain"). It prevents insecure code from being passed to users until the agent fixes the code and it passes review.

CodeShield：为了检查生成的代码是否有漏洞，该模块会利用规则检测代码中不安全的模式，例如容易遭受 SQL 注入攻击（像「SELECT * FROM users WHERE email LIKE '」+ domain +「'」，这种模式允许通过未经净化的输入参数「domain」进行 SQL 注入）。它会阻止不安全代码传递给用户，直到 AI 智能体（AI Agent）修复代码并通过审核。

Results: The authors evaluated LlamaFirewall using AgentDojo, an environment that evaluates attacks against 10 agents (10 different LLMs coupled with the authors' agentic framework).

结果：研究人员使用 AgentDojo 评估了 LlamaFirewall。AgentDojo 是一个专门用于评估针对 10 个 AI 智能体（AI Agent）攻击的环境，这些智能体由 10 个不同的大语言模型（LLM）与研究人员自研的智能体框架相结合而组成。

1 With LlamaFirewall, attacks were successful 1.7 percent of the time. Without it, they succeeded 17.6 percent of the time.

部署 LlamaFirewall 后，攻击的成功率降至 1.7%；而在没有 LlamaFirewall 的情况下，攻击成功率则高达 17.6%。

2 AlignmentCheck detected 83 percent of attacks in a proprietary dataset with a false-positive rate of 2.5 percent.

在一次针对专有数据集（proprietary dataset）的测试中，AlignmentCheck 成功检测出 83% 的攻击，同时其误报率（false-positive rate）仅为 2.5%。

3 The authors tuned PromptGuard 2's classification threshold to achieve a false-positive rate of 1 percent. At this rate, PromptGuard 2 detected 97.5 percent of attacks in a proprietary dataset.

研究人员将 PromptGuard 2 的分类阈值调整到能实现 1% 的误报率（false-positive rate）。在此设置下，PromptGuard 2 在一个专有数据集（proprietary dataset）中成功检测到了 97.5% 的攻击。

4 The authors also compared the performance of PromptGuard 2 to competing prompt classifiers using AgentDojo. With PromptGuard 2, 3.3 percent of jailbreak attempts were successful. Using the next-best competitor, ProtectAI, 13.7 percent succeeded.

研究人员还利用 AgentDojo 平台，对 PromptGuard 2 与其他竞争性的提示词分类器进行了性能比较。结果显示，在使用 PromptGuard 2 时，只有 3.3% 的越狱尝试获得了成功。而排名第二的竞争对手 ProtectAI，其越狱成功率则高达 13.7%。

Why it matters: The rise of agentic systems is opening new vectors of cyberattack, and security risks are likely to rise as agents operate with greater autonomy and perform more critical tasks. LlamaFirewall addresses a wide range of potential security issues in an open-source tool kit.

为何重要：随着 AI 智能体系统（agentic systems）的兴起，新的网络攻击（cyberattack）途径也随之出现。当 AI 智能体（agents）能够更自主地运行并执行更关键的任务时，安全风险预计将大幅增加。LlamaFirewall 提供了一个开源工具包（open-source tool kit），旨在应对各类潜在的安全挑战。

We're thinking: This work is a helpful reminder that, while generative LLMs are all the rage, BERT-style classifiers remain useful when an application needs to classify text quickly.

我们认为，这项工作提供了一个有益的提醒：尽管生成式大语言模型（LLMs）目前炙手可热，但在应用程序需要快速对文本进行分类时，BERT 风格的分类器仍能发挥其重要作用。
