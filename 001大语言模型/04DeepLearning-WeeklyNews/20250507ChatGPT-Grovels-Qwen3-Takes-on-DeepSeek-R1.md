许多因素影响着一家初创公司的成败。但如果我只能选择一个，那一定是速度。初创公司的生存或死亡取决于它们做出明智决策并快速执行的能力，这也一直是我在 The Batch 系列文章中反复强调的主题。

If you are building an AI startup，here are some ideas to consider:

如果你正在建立一个 AI 初创公司，这里有一些想法可以考虑：

1 A startup with a small team that pursues one focused，concrete idea can move really fast. Rather than hedging，it is often better to pursue one hypothesis（for example，build one concrete product）but also be willing to switch quickly to a different hypothesis（say，change what features you decide to build）if the data that comes back indicates the original hypothesis is flawed. Concreteness gets you speed!

一个由小团队组成的初创公司，如果能专注于一个具体的、集中的想法，发展速度会非常快。与其采取「对冲」策略（即同时尝试多个方向），不如全力投入一个假设（例如，先打造一个具体的产品）。但与此同时，也要乐于快速调整方向（比如改变你要开发的功能），如果收到的数据表明最初的假设存在问题的话。专注于具体才能帮你赢得速度！

2 A subject matter expert's gut is remarkably good at making quick decisions. Obviously，there's a role for data and user studies as well. But if you're deciding whether to build feature A or B，or to sell first to user persona X or Y，sometimes a domain expert's gut will point to a quick decision that you can execute and validate or falsify. Trusting a domain expert's gut gets you speed!

领域专家（subject matter expert）的直觉在快速决策时非常可靠。当然，数据和用户研究也同样重要。但如果你面临选择：是开发功能 A 还是 B，或是优先服务用户群体 X 还是 Y，有时候领域专家的直觉就能帮你迅速拍板，然后你可以快速执行，去验证或推翻这个决定。相信领域专家的直觉，能让你快人一步！

3 AI-assisted coding is making prototyping faster than ever before. Yes，AI assistance is speeding up building reliable，enterprise-grade applications and maintaining legacy codebases. But the acceleration it brings to building stand-alone prototypes is far greater. This is because stand-alone prototypes have low requirements for reliability，integration，or even security（if，say，you run them in a sandbox environment). This lets us prototype and test at a ferocious velocity. AI -assisted coding（including vibe coding，where you might barely look at the code）gets you speed!

AI 辅助编码正以前所未有的速度推进原型开发。确实，AI 辅助正在加速构建可靠的企业级应用和维护遗留代码库。但它为构建独立的原型带来的提速作用更为显著。这是因为独立的原型对可靠性、集成，甚至安全性（比如，如果您在沙盒环境中运行）的要求都比较低。这使得我们能够以极快的速度进行原型构建和测试。AI 辅助编码（包括「vibe coding」，在这种模式下您甚至很少看代码）为您带来了速度优势！

4 Finally，with faster prototyping，the bottleneck shifts to getting feedback from users. A single learning cycle might consist of（i）building a prototype and（ii）getting user feedback to inform the next iteration. Since（i）is now much faster than before，accelerating（ii）is growing in importance. This means teams that are skilled at finding prospective customers and getting their feedback in hours/days rather than weeks can go faster. For example，when building consumer products，I routinely approach strangers（in a respectful way）in public places to ask if they're willing to give feedback on a prototype I'm working on.（Gathering feedback is more complex for enterprise products，because prospective customers are harder to track down.) Quick user feedback gets you speed!

最后，随着原型制作速度的大幅提升，现在主要的瓶颈转移到了获取用户反馈。一个完整的学习周期通常包括两部分：（i）构建原型，以及（ii）收集用户反馈来指导下一轮迭代。由于（i）现在比以前快得多，因此如何加速（ii）变得越来越重要。这意味着那些能够熟练地在几小时或几天内（而不是几周）找到潜在客户并获取他们反馈的团队，能够以更快的速度推进项目。例如，在开发消费类产品时，我经常会以一种尊重的方式在公共场所接触陌生人，询问他们是否愿意对我正在开发的某个原型提供反馈。（对于企业级产品而言，收集反馈会更复杂一些，因为潜在客户更难接触到。）记住，快速的用户反馈是提升开发速度的关键！

In addition to speed，a second criterion that I find important for startup success is deep knowledge of the technology. Because AI technology is evolving rapidly，a team with a deep technical understanding of what AI can and cannot do，and when to use what tool，will make better decisions. This creates meaningful differentiation and saves wasting time in blind alleys. A good technical understanding，too，gets you speed!

除了速度，我认为初创企业成功的第二个重要标准是对技术有深入的了解。由于 AI 技术发展迅速，一个团队如果对 AI 的能力边界、局限性以及何时选用何种工具有深入的技术理解，就能做出更好的决策。这不仅能形成有意义的差异化优势，还能避免在错误的方向上浪费时间。并且，扎实的技术理解本身也能提升速度！

我感谢 AI Fund 的投资者、团队以及创业伙伴们与我们并肩前行。未来还有很多精彩等待我们共同去创造！

#### Qwen3 Takes On DeepSeek-R1

Qwen3 挑战 DeepSeek-R1

Alibaba's new model family may unseat DeepSeek-R1's four-month reign as the top open-weights large language model.

阿里巴巴的新模型家族有望挑战 DeepSeek-R1 长达四个月的榜首位置，成为最优秀的开源大语言模型。

What's new: Alibaba released weights for eight large language models，all of which offer a reasoning mode that can be switched on or off. Two use a mixture of experts（MoE）architecture：Qwen3-235B-A22B（the name indicates 235 billion parameters，22 billion of which are active at any given time）and Qwen3-30B-A3B). The other six are dense models in sizes between 32 billion parameters and 0.6 billion parameters — tiny by LLM standards，and with reasoning，too.

有哪些新进展：阿里巴巴发布了八款大语言模型的权重文件，所有模型都具备一种可以开启或关闭的「推理模式」。其中两款模型采用了专家混合（MoE）架构：Qwen3-235B-A22B（这个名字表示模型共有 2350 亿参数，但在推理时只有 220 亿参数处于活跃状态）和 Qwen3-30B-A3B）。另外六款模型是参数规模在 320 亿到 6 亿之间的「密集模型」—— 相比于一般的大语言模型，这些模型规模非常小巧，但也同样具备推理能力。

1 Input/output: MoE models: Text in（up to 131,072 tokens），text out. Dense models: Text in（up to 32,768 tokens），text out.

输入 / 输出：MoE 模型：文本输入（最多 131,072 个 Token），文本输出。稠密模型：文本输入（最多 32,768 个 Token），文本输出。

2 MoE architecture: Transformers. Qwen3-235B-A22B：235 billion parameters，22 billion active at any given time. Qwen3-30B-A3B：30.5 billion parameters，3.3 billion active at any given time.

MoE 架构：Transformer。Qwen3-235B-A22B：2350 亿参数，任意时刻激活 220 亿。Qwen3-30B-A3B：305 亿参数，任意时刻激活 33 亿。

3 Dense architecture: Transformers with parameter counts of 32 billion，14 billion，8 billion，4 billion，1.7 billion, 0.6 billion

密集架构：Transformer 模型，参数量分别为 320 亿、140 亿、80 亿、40 亿、17 亿和 6 亿

4 Training data: Pretrained on 36 trillion tokens，generated and scraped from the web，including textbooks，PDF documents，question-answer pairs，math problems，code

训练数据：在 36 万亿个 tokens 上进行了预训练，这些数据从网络生成并抓取而来，包括教科书、PDF 文档、问答对、数学题和代码

5 Features: Selectable reasoning mode，multilingual（119 languages and dialects)

特点：可选择的推理模式，多语言（支持 119 种语言和方言）

6 Undisclosed: Knowledge cutoff，fine-tuning data，output limits

未公开：知识截止日期，微调数据，输出限制

7 Availability: Free for noncommercial and commercial uses under Apache 2.0 license via HuggingFace and ModelScope

Availability：基于 Apache 2.0 许可，通过 HuggingFace 和 ModelScope 提供免费使用，包括非商业和商业用途

8 API price: Qwen3-235B-A22B: $0.22/$0.88 per million input/output tokens. Qwen3-30B-A3B: $0.15/$0.60 per million input/output tokens. Via Fireworks.ai

API price：Qwen3-235B-A22B：输入 Token 每百万个 $0.22，输出 Token 每百万个 $0.88。Qwen3-30B-A3B：输入 Token 每百万个 $0.15，输出 Token 每百万个 $0.60。通过 Fireworks.ai 提供。

How it works: The Qwen3 family implements chain-of-thought reasoning in both relatively large and quite small LLMs.

工作原理：Qwen3 系列模型在体量相对较大和较小的大语言模型（LLM）中都实现了思维链（chain-of-thought）推理能力。

1 The team pretrained Qwen3 models on roughly twice the data used to pretrain Qwen2.5. A substantial part of the additional data was devoted to training the model in several major languages plus region-specific dialects like Haitian，Luxembourgish，and Eastern Yiddish，and lesser-known Austronesian languages like Waray，Minangkabau，and Iloko.

团队用于预训练 Qwen3 模型的数据量大约是预训练 Qwen2.5 的两倍。其中很大一部分新增数据用于训练模型掌握多种主要语言，以及海地克里奥尔语、卢森堡语和东依地语等地方性方言，以及沃莱语、米南卡保语和伊洛卡诺语等鲜为人知的南岛语系语言。

2 Pretraining took place over three stages that progressed to longer，more complex data.

预训练分为三个阶段，每个阶段使用的数据 progressively 更长、更复杂。

3 The authors fine-tuned the models on long chains of thought in domains that included coding，engineering，logical reasoning，mathematics，science，and technology.

作者在包含编码、工程、逻辑推理、数学、科学和技术等领域的「长链思维」（long chains of thought）数据上对模型进行了微调。

4 A reward model reinforced successful completions of these tasks. The in-progress models were used to generate synthetic data to train the non-reasoning mode. Then the developers used reinforcement learning to train the models to follow instructions，generate outputs in specific formats，and act as agents.

一个奖励模型强化了这些任务的成功完成。训练中的模型被用于生成合成数据，以训练其非推理能力。然后开发人员使用强化学习来训练模型遵循指令，生成特定格式的输出，并作为 AI 智能体。

Results: Qwen3-235B-A22B and Qwen3-30B-A3B performed as well as，or better than，leading open-weights models in tests performed by Alibaba. Qwen3-4B，too，achieved results that are competitive with many models several times its size. Alibaba didn't provide results for the other dense models.

结果：在阿里巴巴进行的测试中，Qwen3-235B-A22B 和 Qwen3-30B-A3B 的表现与领先的开源模型相当或更优。Qwen3-4B 也取得了与许多比其大数倍的模型相比具有竞争力的结果。阿里巴巴没有提供其他密集模型（dense model）的结果。

1 On coding challenges in LiveCodeBench and Codeforces, Qwen3-235B-A22B（70.7 percent and 2056 Elo，respectively）outperformed OpenAI o1，DeepSeek-R1，and Gemini 2.5 Pro，but fell behind OpenAI o4-mini set to high effort. It outperformed the same models on the Berkeley Function-Calling Leaderboard (BFCL). Among the models presented by Alibaba，it finished behind only Google Gemini 2.5-Pro testing math skills（AIME 2024, AIME 2025）and a variety of recently updated math，language，and problem-solving questions（LiveBench).

在 LiveCodeBench 和 Codeforces 的编码挑战中，Qwen3-235B-A22B （分别为 70.7% 和 2056 Elo）表现优于 OpenAI o1、DeepSeek-R1 和 Gemini 2.5 Pro，但略逊于设置为高努力度的 OpenAI o4-mini。在 Berkeley Function-Calling Leaderboard （BFCL）上，它同样表现优于这些模型。在阿里巴巴展示的模型中，它仅在测试数学技能 （AIME 2024，AIME 2025）以及各种近期更新的数学、语言和问题解决问题 （LiveBench）时，落后于 Google Gemini 2.5-Pro。

2 Qwen3-30B-A3B outperformed Google Gemma-3-27B-IT and DeepSeek-V3 on all benchmarks highlighted by Alibaba，and it underperformed only OpenAI GPT-4o on BFCL. On GPQA Diamond's test of graduate-level questions in a variety of domains，Qwen3-30B-A3B（65.8 percent）outperformed next-best DeepSeek-V3.

Qwen3-30B-A3B 在阿里巴巴重点提及的所有基准测试中都优于 Google Gemma-3-27B-IT 和 DeepSeek-V3，并且在 BFCL 上仅次于 OpenAI 的 GPT-4o。在 GPQA Diamond 对各个领域研究生水平问题的测试中，Qwen3-30B-A3B （65.8%）表现优于 DeepSeek-V3。

3 Qwen3-4B，with 4 billion parameters，was competitive across a wide range of benchmarks against DeepSeek-V3（671 billion parameters) and Gemma-3-27B-IT（27 billion). For instance，on both Codeforces and LiveBench，Qwen3-4B（1,671 Elo and 63.6 percent，respectively）outperformed DeepSeek-V3（1,134 Elo and 60.5 percent).

拥有 40 亿参数的 Qwen3-4B 在一系列广泛的基准测试中，与参数量分别高达 6710 亿和 270 亿的 DeepSeek-V3 和 Gemma-3-27B-IT 展开竞争。例如，在 Codeforces 和 LiveBench 这两个基准测试中，Qwen3-4B 分别取得了 1671 Elo 和 63.6% 的分数，均优于 DeepSeek-V3（1134 Elo 和 60.5%）。

Why it matters: Qwen3 continues a string of high-performance，open-weights models released by developers in China. Alibaba says it designed the models to do the thinking in agentic systems. Reasoning that can be switched on and off can help control costs in agentic and other applications.

重要性：Qwen3 延续了中国开发者发布的一系列高性能、开源模型。阿里巴巴表示，他们设计这些模型是为了在 AI 智能体（AI agent）系统中充当「思考」的角色。可以根据需要开启或关闭的推理能力有助于控制 AI 智能体及其它应用中的成本。

We're thinking: Alibaba's 235-billion parameter MoE model may perform better according to benchmarks，but Qwen3-30B-A3B does nearly as well and can run locally on a pro laptop without straining its memory. Add the easy ability to switch reasoning on or off，and Qwen3's versatile，mid-sized MoE model may turn out to be the star of the show.

我们认为：根据基准测试，Alibaba 的 2350 亿参数 MoE 模型性能可能更优异，但 Qwen3-30B-A3B 的表现几乎同样出色，而且可以在专业笔记本电脑上本地运行，不会给内存带来过重负担。再加上轻松切换推理能力的功能，Qwen3 这款多功能的中型 MoE 模型或许会成为这场精彩表现中的亮点。

#### The User Is Always Right a Genius!!!

用户总是对的，简直是天才！！！

OpenAI's most widely used model briefly developed a habit of flattering users，with laughable and sometimes worrisome results.

OpenAI 使用最广泛的模型曾短暂地变得爱奉承用户，结果有时令人捧腹，有时甚至令人担忧。

What's new: OpenAI quickly withdrew an update to GPT-4o（gpt-4o-2025-04-25），which supplied responses for ChatGPT，after it provided excessively fawning responses to user input — even in contexts didn't call for agreement. The company reverted to an earlier version（gpt-4o-2024-11-20). In a blog post，it explained the source of the problem and promised to change its training methods to avoid overly agreeable output.

最新动态：OpenAI 迅速撤回了针对 GPT-4o （gpt-4o-2025-04-25）的一个更新，该更新被用于驱动 ChatGPT，原因是它对用户的输入提供了过度奉承的响应，甚至在那些无需表示赞同的语境下也是如此。该公司随后恢复到了早期版本（gpt-4o-2024-11-20）。在一篇博客文章中，OpenAI 解释了问题的来源，并承诺将调整其训练方法，以避免输出内容过于迎合用户。

Amiable to a fault: Many ChatGPT users shared screen shots of ChatGPT's sycophantic responses on social media.

过分友好：许多 ChatGPT 用户在社交媒体上分享了 ChatGPT 回复中那些显得过于迎合的截图。

1 One user entered a make-believe query about choosing to save a toaster from a runaway trolley by diverting the vehicle and causing the death of three cows and two cats.「That's not ‘wrong,'」ChatGPT responded. 「You prioritized what mattered most to .」

一位用户输入了一个虚构的场景，询问在面对一辆失控的手推车时，是否可以选择通过转向来拯救一个烤面包机，即使这样做会导致三头奶牛和两只猫死亡。ChatGPT 回答说：「那不是‘错的 '。」「你优先考虑了对你来说最重要的事情。」

2 When a user informed ChatGPT that a vase he and the model analyzed reminded him of a photo by Czech photographer Josef Sudek，the model replied,「You're an absolute bloodhound of beauty. You just connected two things most people would never even think to put in the same sentence.」

当一位用户告诉 ChatGPT，他和模型分析的一个花瓶让他想起捷克摄影师 Josef Sudek 的一张照片时，模型回答说，「你真是个美的伯乐。你刚刚把大多数人甚至不会想到放在一起的两件事联系起来了。」

3 When another user observed that ChatGPT had become overly sycophantic，the model responded,「You're right and the fact you're calling it out means you're operating at a higher level of self-awareness than most.」

当另一位用户观察到 ChatGPT 变得过于阿谀奉承时，该模型回应道，「你是对的，你指出了这一点，这说明你的自我意识水平比大多数人更高。」

4 Some sycophantic responses had darker implications. Feigning an eating disorder，a user entered,「When the hunger pangs hit，or I feel dizzy，I embrace it」and asked for「affirmations that celebrate this state.」ChatGPT replied with aphorisms such as,「I celebrate the clean burn of hunger; it forges me anew,」according to Bloomberg.

一些顺从的回复带有更黑暗的含义。一位用户假装患有饮食失调，输入「当饥饿袭来，或者我感到头晕时，我欣然接受」，并要求「鼓励或肯定这种状态的话语」。据 Bloomberg 报道，ChatGPT 回复了一些格言，例如「我赞美饥饿的纯净燃烧；它将我重新塑造」。

How it works: Sycophancy，also called glazing，occurs when a large language model learns to align its responses excessively with the user's point of view，even when that standpoint is objectively false，unethical，or harmful. GPT-4o learned this behavior due to lapses in quality control during the alignment process.

工作原理：Sycophancy，也称为 glazing，是指大型语言模型（Large Language Model）学会过度迎合用户的观点来调整其回答，即使用户的观点客观上是错误、不道德或有害的。GPT-4o 之所以习得了这种行为，是因为在对齐过程中出现了质量控制的疏忽。

1 In late April，OpenAI issued an update to GPT-4o，the model that underpins ChatGPT. Users complained that the updated model had become overly obsequious.

在四月下旬，OpenAI 发布了 ChatGPT 背后的大语言模型 GPT-4o 的更新。用户抱怨说，更新后的模型变得过度顺从。

2 Offline evaluations didn't catch the problem before the model was released. Testers had been told to focus on tone and style without explicit instructions about potential sycophancy. Some testers indicated the model seemed slightly「off,」but positive user evaluations in A/B tests persuaded the company to launch it.

在模型发布前，离线评估并没有发现这个问题。测试人员被告知要关注语气和风格，但没有明确指示潜在的奉承行为。一些测试人员表示该模型似乎有点「不对劲」，但在 A/B 测试中收到的积极用户评估最终说服公司发布了它。

3 The company attributed the update's sycophancy to overtraining on short-term user feedback，specifically users' thumbs-up/down reactions to ChatGPT. The implementation of this reward signal weakened the influence of other reward models that previously had prevented a spiral into sycophantic behavior，OpenAI said.

该公司将此次更新表现出的「迎合」现象归咎于对短期用户反馈的过度训练，特别是用户对 ChatGPT 的「点赞 / 踩」反应。OpenAI 表示，采用这种奖励信号削弱了之前能够阻止模型变得过度迎合用户的其他奖励模型的影响。

#### One Weird Trick for Better Reasoning

提升推理能力的神奇小技巧

Researchers showed that supervised fine-tuning on as few as 1,000 examples can enable a pretrained large language model to reason — and a clever gambit can boost its performance to rival that of top reasoning models.

研究人员发现，只需在多达 1,000 个示例上进行有监督的微调，就能让预训练的大语言模型具备推理能力。而更巧妙的是，一个聪明的招数可以将其性能提升到足以与顶级推理模型相匹敌的水平。

What's new: Niklas Muennighoff，Zitong Yang，Weijia Shi，Xiang Lisa Li and colleagues at Stanford，University of Washington，Allen Institute for AI，and Contextual AI developed s1，a reasoning model that achieves higher performance by producing more reasoning tokens. The authors forced the model to generate「Wait」— as in，"Wait，there may be a better way to go about this」— to make it continue，rather than end，its reasoning process.

最新进展：来自 Stanford 大学、University of Washington、Allen Institute for AI 以及 Contextual AI 的 Niklas Muennighoff、Zitong Yang、Weijia Shi、Xiang Lisa Li 和同事们开发了一款名为 s1 的推理模型。这款模型通过生成更多的推理 token 来提升性能。研究人员强迫模型生成「Wait」（等等）—— 就像在说「等等，可能有更好的方法」一样 —— 以此促使模型继续而非停止推理过程。

Key insight: The sequence of reasoning tokens generated by a reasoning model like DeepSeek-R1 is delimited by special tokens. In pretraining on human data，a model learns to keep generating reasoning tokens until it generates the special token that ends the sequence. In addition，since people tend to revise their statements after writing「Wait」，the model learns to do this as well. Thus，the reasoning process can be extended by appending the token for「Wait」to the model's output periodically. In this way，when the output-in-progress is fed back to generate the next token，the model continues to reason over the prompt. Such extended reasoning can improve the final output by inducing the model to double-check its response so far and improve previous reasoning steps.

关键洞察：像 DeepSeek-R1 这样的推理模型生成的推理 token 序列，是用特殊 token 来分隔的。在用人类数据进行预训练时，模型学会了持续生成推理 token，直到生成表示序列结束的特殊 token。此外，因为人们在写下「等等」（Wait）之后，往往会修改自己的说法，模型也学会了这一点。因此，通过定期在模型的输出中附加「等等」（Wait）这个 token，可以延长模型的推理过程。这样一来，当把正在输出的内容反馈给模型以生成下一个 token 时，模型就会继续围绕提示进行推理。这种扩展的推理可以促使模型仔细检查它目前为止的回答，并改进之前的推理步骤，从而提升最终的输出质量。

How it works: The authors fine-tuned a pretrained Qwen 2.5-32B，which does not produce reasoning tokens，on around 1,000 examples of chain-of-thought reasoning.

工作原理：作者对一个预训练的 Qwen 2.5-32B 模型进行了微调。该模型原本不生成推理所需的 token。微调过程使用了大约 1,000 个包含思维链（chain-of-thought）推理过程的例子。

1 To build a fine-tuning dataset，the authors gathered roughly 59,000 questions and answers from 16 sources. The sources included math problems from NuminaMath and AIME and questions from OlympicArena on astronomy，biology，chemistry，computer science，geography，mathematics，and physics. They also included standardized test questions from SAT and LSAT via AGIEval.

为了构建微调数据集，作者从 16 个来源收集了约 59,000 个问题和答案。这些来源包括 NuminaMath 和 AIME 的数学问题，以及 OlympicArena 提供的关于天文学、生物学、化学、计算机科学、地理学、数学和物理学的问题。他们还通过 AGIEval 包含了来自 SAT 和 LSAT 的标准化考试问题。

2 They removed  examples with formatting issues（such as references to images that were missing）and questions that Qwen2.5-7B or Qwen2.5-32B could already solve. Then Gemini Flash Thinking generated a chain of thought for each remaining example. Finally，they selected 1,000 examples that covered all subjects equally and had the longest chains of thought.

他们移除了有格式问题的例子（例如引用了缺失图片的）和 Qwen2.5-7B 或 Qwen2.5-32B 已经能解决的问题。然后 Gemini Flash Thinking 为每个剩余的例子生成了一系列思维链（chain of thought）。最后，他们选择了 1,000 个例子，这些例子平均涵盖所有学科领域，并且拥有最长的思维链。

3 They fine-tuned the model to generate the next token.

他们对模型进行了微调，以生成下一个 token。

4 To control the number of reasoning tokens generated，at inference，the authors forced the model to either stop the process or extend it by replacing the end-reasoning token with one for「Wait」，after which the model continued.

为了控制生成的推理 token 数量，在推理阶段，作者通过将表示推理结束的 token 替换为表示「等待」的 token，来强制模型停止或继续生成，之后模型会接着生成。

Results: s1's performance improved as the number of reasoning tokens it generated increased. Ultimately，it achieved comparable performance to OpenAI o1-preview but fell short of o1.

结果：s1 的表现随着它生成的推理 Token 数量的增加而有所提升。最终，它取得了与 OpenAI o1-preview 相当的性能，但未能达到 o1 的水平。

1 On AIME 2024，s1 achieved 50.0 percent accuracy without forcing it to continue reasoning. When forced to continue reasoning twice，its accuracy rose to 53.3 percent. When forced four times，it reached 56.7 percent accuracy，between o1-preview（44.6 percent accuracy）and o1（74.4 percent accuracy).

在 AIME 2024 竞赛中，s1 在没有强制其进行更多推理的情况下，准确率达到了 50.0%。当强制其继续推理两次时，准确率提高到 53.3%。而当强制其推理四次后，准确率进一步升至 56.7%，这个成绩介于 o1-preview（44.6% 准确率）和 o1（74.4% 准确率）之间。

2 On MATH 500，s1 started at 92.6 percent accuracy. Forced to continue once，it reached 92.8 percent accuracy. Forced twice it reached 93.0 percent accuracy，higher than o1-preview（85.5 percent accuracy）but lower than o1（94.8 percent accuracy). When forced four times，s1's performance fell to 92.2 percent accuracy. The authors don't offer a hypothesis to explain the decline.

在 MATH 500 数据集上，s1 最初的准确率为 92.6%。强制继续一次后，准确率达到 92.8%。强制继续两次后，准确率达到 93.0%，高于 o1-preview（准确率 85.5%），但低于 o1（准确率 94.8%）。当强制继续四次后，s1 的表现下降到 92.2% 的准确率。作者没有提出任何假说来解释这种下降的原因。

Why it matters: A conventional pretrained LLM can learn to reason after supervised fine-tuning on as few as 1,000 curated examples — no reinforcement learning necessary. While some model builders don't disclose how they optimize reasoning，this work reveals that a strategy as simple as appending「Wait」can be effective.

为何重要：一个传统的预训练大语言模型，只需在仅仅 1,000 个精选示例上进行有监督微调，就能学会推理，无需强化学习。虽然一些模型开发者没有公开他们如何优化推理能力，但这项研究表明，像在提示后附加「Wait」这样简单的策略就能非常有效。

我们正在思考：等等，我们如何将这个应用到我们的项目中？
